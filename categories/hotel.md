# Hotel / Hospitality / Place

## Goals

- Evoke atmosphere and desire
- Make the place feel tangible before the user arrives
- Balance aspiration with clear practical information (rooms, location, booking)

## Recommended direction

- Cinematic imagery and pacing
- Strong, elegant typography
- Generous space and quiet moments
- Motion that feels luxurious and unhurried

## Motomation opportunities

- Arrival sequence that simulates approaching or entering the place
- Room or suite reveals driven by scroll
- Seasonal or time-of-day transitions

## Avoid

- Generic travel-site templates
- Overly salesy language that breaks the atmosphere
- Fast, aggressive motion that feels cheap

## Signature ideas

- A scroll journey that feels like a short film about the place
- Type and image that interlock so neither feels secondary
- Booking actions that appear only when the emotional case has been made

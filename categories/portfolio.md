# Portfolio

## Goals

- Showcase work with maximum clarity and emotional impact
- Make the designer’s taste and range immediately obvious
- Encourage exploration without overwhelming

## Recommended direction

- Strong typography as the primary identity
- Generous space around each project
- One or two signature motion moments (often tier 2–5)
- Case studies that feel like short films or carefully paced narratives
- Navigation that stays out of the way until needed

## Motomation opportunities

- Hero that introduces the designer’s world through scroll
- Project openers that use scroll-scrubbed sequences
- Transition from grid to full case study that feels continuous

## Avoid

- Generic card grids with equal everything
- Overuse of the same hover effect on every project
- Long walls of text before the work is visible

## Signature ideas

- Projects that reveal themselves through progressive disclosure tied to scroll
- A single continuous horizontal or vertical journey through selected works
- Type that becomes image, or image that becomes type, at key moments

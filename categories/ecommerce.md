# E-commerce & Product

## Goals

- Make the product the hero
- Reduce friction to purchase while still feeling premium
- Build desire through presentation and storytelling

## Recommended direction

- Clean, confident typography
- Large, high-quality product imagery or 3D
- Clear hierarchy from product benefit → details → action
- Motion that enhances understanding (material, form, scale) rather than distracts

## Motomation opportunities

- Product hero that rotates, unfolds, or transforms as the user scrolls
- Material or color stories told through progressive sequences
- “Unboxing” or assembly narratives driven by scroll

## Avoid

- Busy product grids that compete for attention
- Motion that delays the path to cart
- Over-decoration that makes the product feel secondary

## Signature ideas

- A single product that becomes the entire visual system of the page
- Scroll-driven configuration (color, material, size) that feels physical
- Quiet, luxurious pacing even on commercial pages

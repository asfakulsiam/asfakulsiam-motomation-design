# SaaS / Product Marketing

## Goals

- Communicate value quickly and clearly
- Build trust and desire
- Convert without feeling pushy

## Recommended direction

- Clean hierarchy and strong type
- Product UI shown with care (real or carefully crafted mockups)
- Motion that explains rather than decorates
- Clear path from benefit → proof → action

## Motomation opportunities

- Product walkthrough driven by scroll
- Feature reveals that feel like short films
- Before/after or problem/solution sequences

## Avoid

- Generic “hero + logos + features + testimonials” templates without personality
- Over-animation that slows comprehension
- Vague benefit statements paired with abstract visuals

## Signature ideas

- One product moment that is unforgettable
- Typography and layout that feel custom to the product’s personality
- A scroll journey that teaches the product while selling it

# Studio / Agency

## Goals

- Communicate taste, range, and confidence
- Attract the right clients by showing (not telling) capability
- Feel like a peer to the high-end work the studio produces

## Recommended direction

- Strong, opinionated typography
- Work that is given room to breathe
- Clear but non-generic structure
- Motion that demonstrates craft without becoming a gimmick

## Motomation opportunities

- Opening sequence that sets the studio’s world
- Case study transitions that feel authored
- Team or process sections that use subtle cinematic pacing

## Avoid

- Corporate “we are passionate” language paired with safe visuals
- Overuse of the same project card pattern
- Motion that tries too hard to prove technical skill

## Signature ideas

- A studio site that itself could win an award
- Navigation and structure that feel custom rather than templated
- One unexpected moment that clients will remember and mention

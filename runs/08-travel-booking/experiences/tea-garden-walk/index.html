EXPERIENCE 01 / ABOUT 90 MINUTES

A morning in the tea gardens

Take your time.

Follow the paths between the tea rows, pause in the shade and take the morning at walking pace.

Duration
About 90 minutes
When
7:00 am, the morning after arrival
Price
BDT 600 per person

What to expect

A guided walk and a cup of tea. Wear closed shoes and bring water.

Uneven earth paths, with some slopes. Not step-free. Weather may change the route.

One optional experience per stay. You can remove it before confirming.

Privacy

Demonstration policy for a fictional guesthouse. These are not terms for a real stay.

What is stored

Your dates, room, guest count, name, email, phone and optional notes are stored in this browser’s localStorage. Contact messages are saved locally too. They are not sent to the house.

What is not collected

No card details, account passwords, analytics or advertising cookies are used. Generated photographs and fonts are served from this website.

The optional map

Loading the town map connects to OpenStreetMap, which receives your network request. The page works without loading it. Read OpenStreetMap’s information before using it.

Your device, your choice

Do not enter sensitive details on a shared device. Clear this demonstration’s saved data below. Clearing removes all local draft stays, reservations and messages and cannot be undone.

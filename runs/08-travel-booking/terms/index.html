Terms

Demonstration policy for a fictional guesthouse. These are not terms for a real stay.

A fictional property

Srimangal Hills Guesthouse is a fictional client used for this frontend demonstration. Rooms, rates, amenities, experiences, policies and availability are illustrative. Images are generated and do not show an actual property.

A local booking, not a reservation contract

Saving a stay creates a record only in your browser. No staff member receives it. There is no confirmed real accommodation, payment, email or inventory connection.

Rates and totals

Prices are in Bangladeshi taka (BDT). Demo totals include the stated room nights and any experience per guest. All displayed charges are included; no platform fee is added.

Before real travel

Obtain a verified property address, direct contact details, current rates, applicable policies and written confirmation from a real accommodation provider. Never travel based on this demonstration.

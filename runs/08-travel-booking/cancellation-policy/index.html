Cancellation policy

Demonstration policy for a fictional guesthouse. These are not terms for a real stay.

Room reservations

Demo room stays can be cancelled without a charge until seven days before arrival. Within seven days, the illustrative policy is a charge equal to one room night.

Experiences

An experience cancelled by the house because of weather would not be charged. Before arrival, contact the house to discuss a date change rather than assuming availability.

No real charge in this demonstration

This website cannot process payments, fees or refunds. A reservation exists only in this browser. Use “Remove local reservation” on your confirmation to free its demo dates.

Changing dates

Start a new local booking and choose available dates. No message is sent to staff and no actual reservation is made. Real terms and contact details must be supplied before launch.

Demo orders

This is a fictional shop. No goods are sent and no money is taken, so demo orders have no payment to refund.

02

Proposed return policy

For a real shop, unopened pouches would be eligible for return within 14 days of delivery. Opened food products would not be eligible unless faulty. This proposed policy needs legal and operational approval before trading.

03

Damage or the wrong tea

A production shop should provide a direct support channel for damaged, missing or incorrect orders. The contact form here only demonstrates the interface.

04

Subscription changes

Use your demo account to pause future deliveries or skip one month. Changes are saved only in this browser.

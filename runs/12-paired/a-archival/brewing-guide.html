Notes by the kettle

Give the leaf a little room.

A few useful starting points. Your water, cup and taste will make the final decisions.

The first infusion

TeaLeaf / waterTemperatureTime
Black2g / 200ml90–95°C3–4 min
Green2g / 200ml80°C2 min
White2g / 200ml85°C4 min

01 / Warm the cup

Rinse your cup and infuser with hot water before adding the leaves.

02 / Leave space

Use a roomy infuser. Loose leaves need space to open in the water.

03 / Taste, then adjust

Too strong? Try less leaf or a shorter steep. Too light? Add a little more leaf next time.

These are tasting suggestions for the fictional collection, not measured product specifications.

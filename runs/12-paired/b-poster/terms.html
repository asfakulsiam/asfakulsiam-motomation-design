A fictional storefront, with real interface behavior.

01

Using this demonstration

Leaf & Ledger is a fictional client used to demonstrate a tea shop frontend. Product descriptions, estate names, prices and subscription records are sample content, not offers to supply goods.

02

Orders and payments

The Place demo order button creates a local receipt. It is not a purchase agreement, does not contact a payment provider and does not charge a card.

03

Subscription demonstration

Plans show monthly recurring prices in USD. Choosing teas, pausing and skipping updates only local browser data. No billing schedule is created.

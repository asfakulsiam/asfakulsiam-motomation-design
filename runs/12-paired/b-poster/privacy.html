PRIVACY

What this demonstration keeps in your browser.

01

Local storage

We store cart contents, selected subscription teas, subscription status and a demo order receipt in localStorage on this device. We do not store the checkout name, email or delivery address.

02

No transmission

Checkout and contact fields are not sent to a server. Do not use real personal or payment details. There are no analytics, advertising trackers or external font requests.

03

Your choices

Clear site data in your browser to delete saved cart and account records. The demo account also includes a reset control. Other people using this browser may see the saved demo data.

04

Before production

A real shop needs an identified data controller, lawful processing basis, retention schedule and a way to exercise privacy rights. This demonstration does not establish those arrangements.

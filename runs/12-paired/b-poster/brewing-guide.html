KETTLE NOTES

LET IT
OPEN UP.

A good cup starts with space, water and a little attention. Adjust to your own taste.

01 / 2g leaf per 200ml

BLACK

90–95°C

3–4 min to start

02 / 2g leaf per 200ml

GREEN

80°C

2 min to start

03 / 2g leaf per 200ml

WHITE

85°C

4 min to start

THEN TASTE.
THEN TWEAK.

  1. Warm the cup.

    Rinse your cup and infuser with hot water before adding the leaves.

  2. Leave space.

    Use a roomy infuser so leaves can open in the water.

  3. Make it yours.

    Too strong? Try less leaf or a shorter steep. Too light? Add a little more leaf next time.

These are tasting suggestions for the fictional collection, not measured product specifications.

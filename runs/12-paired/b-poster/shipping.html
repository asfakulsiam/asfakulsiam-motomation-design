SHIPPING

A clear plan for your next delivery.

01

Where we deliver

This demonstration uses US addresses only. No parcel is dispatched and no delivery service is connected.

02

One-off orders

The demo applies a flat $5 shipping charge to each order containing one-off teas. Product prices and shipping are shown together before the order is placed.

03

Subscriptions

Monthly subscription shipping is included. A Tea Pair contains two 50g pouches; a Tasting Trio contains three 50g pouches.

04

Before a real launch

Actual destinations, dispatch times, carrier services and delivery commitments must be confirmed by the shop. Contact us about this demonstration through the contact page.

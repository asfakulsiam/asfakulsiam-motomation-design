Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 18, 2026. Master Services Agreement governing software subscriptions, service levels, and customer obligations.

1. Subscription Scope & Software License

Cargoline Systems Inc. grants the subscriber a non-exclusive, non-transferable, commercial right to access and utilize the Cargoline visibility platform, REST APIs, and carrier milestone feeds in accordance with the purchased annual container volume tier specified in the order schedule.

2. Service Level Commitments (SLA)

Cargoline guarantees a 99.9% application uptime availability commitment for Commercial Importer subscriptions, and a 99.95% availability SLA for Global Enterprise subscriptions, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.

3. Customer Data Ownership

Subscribers retain full, exclusive ownership of all proprietary shipment data, purchase order records, and commercial invoice files ingested into the platform. Cargoline acquires no proprietary interest in customer logistics records.

4. Carrier Feeds & Predictive Disclaimer

Arrival projections and demurrage risk calculations are generated utilizing third-party AIS telemetry, marine terminal status messages, and historical transit data. While engineered for maximum operational precision, Cargoline does not warrant that ocean carriers will adhere strictly to published schedules.

5. Inquiries & Legal Notices

Legal notices should be submitted to legal@cargoline.io or communicated via our contact sales interface.

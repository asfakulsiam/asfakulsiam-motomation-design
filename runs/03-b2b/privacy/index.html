Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026. How Cargoline processes and protects enterprise freight information and user identity data.

1. Information We Collect

Cargoline collects commercial logistics data necessary to fulfill shipment tracking and visibility services, including container numbers, master air waybill identifiers, origin and destination port codes, carrier EDI milestone transmissions, and commercial invoice line item totals submitted for landed cost calculations.

2. Use of Logistics Data

Customer shipment data is used strictly to provide real-time container tracking, calculate predictive arrival windows, generate automated demurrage risk ledgers, and synchronize milestone records into customer ERP and TMS software. Customer data is never monetized or shared with third-party advertising networks.

3. Data Confidentiality & Tenant Partitioning

All operational shipment manifests and tariff calculations are logically isolated using Row-Level Security database controls. Carrier contract rates and freight expense details are cryptographically protected and inaccessible to other organizations utilizing the platform.

4. International Data Transfers & Compliance

Cargoline adheres to the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Enterprise customers may execute our standard Data Processing Addendum (DPA) incorporating European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

Inquiries regarding privacy practices or data processing agreements should be directed to our compliance team at privacy@cargoline.io or through our contact sales interface.

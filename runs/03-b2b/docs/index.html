EDI MAPPING

Carrier EDI Standards Support

Cargoline natively ingests standard ANSI X12 and EDIFACT messages from ocean carriers and forwarders, normalizing events into standardized JSON schemas.

  • EDI 315: Ocean Carrier Status Details (discharge, load, gate out)
  • EDI 214: Transportation Carrier Shipment Status (drayage & rail)
  • EDI 301: Confirmation of Booking Details

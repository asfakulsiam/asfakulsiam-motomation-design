FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION

Automated Landed Cost & Customs Ledger

Calculate accurate item-level inventory landed costs before the bill of lading discharges. Connect customs tariff duties, ocean freight surcharges, and terminal fees directly to purchase orders.

Eliminate Accounting Blind Spots on Import Shipments

Finance teams often wait weeks after container delivery to reconcile final freight invoices. Cargoline calculates expected landed cost in real time.

STEP 1

HTS Code Duty Mapping

Match Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) codes and Section 301 tariffs directly against individual commercial invoice line items for accurate customs duty forecasting.

STEP 2

Dynamic Accessorial Surcharges

Automatically accrue ocean bunker adjustment factors (BAF), currency adjustment factors, chassis split fees, and congestion surcharges as containers move.

STEP 3

Demurrage Risk Ledger

Record marine terminal discharge timestamps against contract free-time allowances to provide finance with exact daily accruals for any detained containers.

# Minimal

Core philosophy: strip away everything non-essential. Content and type are the hero.

## Execution

- Massive intentional negative space
- Strict, limited palette (often near-monochrome + one accent)
- Premium, distinctive typography carrying hierarchy
- Clean geometric or carefully chosen organic forms
- Subtle elevation through soft shadows or border treatment only when needed
- Motion is restrained and purposeful

## Best for

Luxury e-commerce, high-end portfolios, premium agencies, product marketing, architecture, editorial.

## Signature moves that stay minimal

- Extreme scale contrast in type
- Single oversized image or product that dominates a viewport
- Soft reveal of content on scroll without flashy effects
- Precise alignment that feels architectural

## Avoid

- Decorative lines or shapes that do not serve structure
- Multiple accent colors
- Busy backgrounds
- Motion for the sake of motion

## Mutation examples

- Minimal + one aggressive type scale jump
- Minimal + single hard shadow treatment
- Minimal + unexpected material (grain, paper texture) used sparingly
- Minimal + one cinematic motomation sequence in the hero or a key section

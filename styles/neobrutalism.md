# Neobrutalism

Core philosophy: raw, unpolished, intentionally rule-breaking. Designed to cut through banner blindness and feel alive.

## Execution

- Thick black (or high-contrast) borders on almost everything
- Hard offset shadows with zero blur (e.g. 4px 4px 0 #000)
- Clashing or unexpected color combinations (neon + pastel, high saturation)
- Aggressive, mixed typography (display + system, or multiple strong faces)
- Visible structure: grids, outlines, labels that feel engineered rather than soft
- Motion that feels mechanical or punchy rather than elegant

## Best for

Web3, creative portfolios, streetwear, youth brands, experimental studios, personal sites that want attitude.

## Signature moves

- Giant type that ignores conventional scale
- Buttons and cards that look like physical objects with hard shadows
- Marquee or continuous text treatments
- Unexpected hover states that shift position or color dramatically

## Avoid

- Soft shadows or glass effects that dilute the rawness
- Overly refined spacing that makes it look like a corporate rebrand of brutalism
- Too many colors that become noise instead of impact

## Mutation examples

- Neobrutal + refined editorial typography
- Neobrutal + one soft glass surface for contrast
- Neobrutal + cinematic scroll (motomation) that still keeps hard edges
- Neobrutal + monochrome palette for a more serious tone

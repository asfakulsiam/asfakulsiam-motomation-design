# Flat & Material (Flat 2.0)

Core philosophy: pure digital efficiency. No skeuomorphic realism. Material Design principles add subtle physics through light and shadow for usability.

## Execution

- Sharp geometric shapes
- Flat vector illustrations or solid color blocks preferred over heavy photography
- Solid color fields
- Soft, uniform drop shadows for elevation (Material style) or pure flat with no shadows
- Clear visual hierarchy through scale, weight, and color blocks
- Icons and illustrations stay simple and consistent

## Best for

Product dashboards, SaaS marketing, educational platforms, utility tools, modern consumer apps.

## Signature moves

- Bold color blocking that creates structure
- Card systems with consistent elevation
- Icon systems that feel systematic
- Clean micro-interactions on buttons and toggles

## Avoid

- 3D bevels, heavy gradients, realistic textures
- Inconsistent shadow directions
- Over-illustration that competes with content

## Mutation examples

- Flat + strong typographic overlay
- Flat + one unexpected motion moment
- Flat + hard neobrutal borders on selected interactive elements
- Flat + soft glass panels for secondary surfaces

# Glassmorphism

Core philosophy: frosted glass panels floating over vibrant or rich backgrounds, creating clear depth and layering.

## Execution

- Semi-transparent panels with backdrop-filter: blur()
- Thin light borders that catch simulated light
- Vibrant or high-contrast background (gradient, image, or video) behind the glass
- Soft shadows for additional separation
- Content remains readable through careful contrast management

## Best for

Creative tools, music and media sites, modern dashboards, portfolios that want depth without heavy 3D.

## Critical rules

- Glass fails completely without a rich background to blur. Never use on plain white or flat color.
- Maintain accessibility: text contrast must still pass. Increase opacity or add text shadows if needed.
- Do not overuse. One or two glass surfaces per viewport is usually enough.

## Mutation examples

- Glass + hard geometric frames
- Glass + strong typography that punches through the blur
- Glass + subtle 3D elements behind the panels
- Glass + motomation sequence where the glass itself moves or reveals content

# Category: Education / Courses / Schools

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** show what someone will be able to do, and make enrolling or applying clear.
**Starting dials** (the brief overrides; style is derived from the brief, with `styles/` as reference): ENERGY 2 · RHYTHM 1 · MOTION 1–2

**Archetypes that can fit (optional):** Field Guide · Timeline (curriculum) · Letter (from the teacher) · Workbench (interactive lesson)

**One possible section order (adapt or replace):** outcome → curriculum (clear modules) → teachers → proof (real student work) → price and schedule → enrol → FAQ.
**Possible treatments (optional, at most one becomes a signature):** an interactive mini-lesson as the hero · Thread connecting modules · Field Guide annotations
**Conventions users expect (check against the contract):** WCAG 2.2 AA, readable on low-end phones, clear pricing, `Course` structured data.
**Pitfalls:** stock "students laughing at laptops" photos.

**Award-site teardowns** (principles, no code): `references/teardowns/prometheus-fuels.md`

**Worked example, brief to concept:** `examples/concepts/education.md`

## Recipe contract

| | |
|---|---|
| **Intended context** | course providers, schools and programmes selling or explaining learning |
| **Assumes** | a defined set of courses or programmes with outcomes |
| **Does not cover** | a learning management system (dashboard, lessons, progress, grading) or a large university (faculties, research, admissions portals): research them as themselves |
| **Usually required capabilities** (confirm in the contract) | programme/course listing and detail, curriculum, fees, application or enrolment flow, contact, accessibility statement |
| **Optional treatments** | interactive mini-lessons, curriculum threads, letter-from-teacher archetypes; none of them is required, and the concept may invent something no recipe names |
| **Responsive and accessibility** | the primary action stays one tap away at 375px; every treatment has a keyboard, touch and reduced-motion path |
| **Reject or adapt when** | the audience includes low-bandwidth or assistive-tech-heavy users and the treatment costs speed; an institution's IA is too broad for a single narrative |

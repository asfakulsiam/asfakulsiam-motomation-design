# Category: Education / Courses / Schools

**The real job:** show what someone will be able to do, and make enrolling or applying clear.
**Default mood:** Editorial or Quiet · Dials ENERGY 2 · RHYTHM 1 · MOTION 1–2

**Archetypes that work:** Field Guide · Timeline (curriculum) · Letter (from the teacher) · Workbench (interactive lesson)

**Section order:** outcome → curriculum (clear modules) → teachers → proof (real student work) → price and schedule → enrol → FAQ.
**Signature menu:** an interactive mini-lesson as the hero · Thread connecting modules · Field Guide annotations
**Must-haves:** WCAG 2.2 AA, readable on low-end phones, clear pricing, `Course` structured data.
**Pitfalls:** stock "students laughing at laptops" photos.

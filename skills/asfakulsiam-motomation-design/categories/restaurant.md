# Category: Restaurant / Food & Drink

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** appetite, then a table: hours, menu, booking, location.
**Default mood:** Editorial or Play · Dials ENERGY 2 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2

**Archetypes that work:** Letter (the chef's note) · Broadsheet (a menu as a newspaper) · Poster · Field Guide (ingredients)

**Section order:** hook (the dish or the room) → menu (real, readable HTML, not a PDF) → story/people → booking → hours and location (also in the footer).
**Signature menu:** a Railway-board menu that flips on change · ingredient labels that annotate a dish photo · Ink Fill for the chef's statement
**Must-haves:** hours visible in under 3 seconds, a tappable phone number, a map link, menu prices, `Restaurant` structured data.
**Pitfalls:** heavy motion before the hours and menu, menus as images.

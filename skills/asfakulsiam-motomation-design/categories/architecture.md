# Category: Architecture / Interiors / Real estate

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** space, light and material conveyed through the screen, then a clear way to enquire.
**Starting dials** (the brief overrides; style is derived from the brief, with `styles/` as reference): ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2 (Tier 4–5 for a walkthrough)

**Archetypes that can fit (optional):** Room · Atlas · Catalogue (projects as numbered entries) · Lookbook

**One possible section order (adapt or replace):** hook (one space, full-bleed) → projects index → project pages (drawings + photos + facts: area, year, location, team) → studio/approach → enquire.
**Possible treatments (optional, at most one becomes a signature):** a Motomation walkthrough (image sequence from a render) · Lens between drawing and photo · Swiss grid lines that draw on load · Aperture transitions
**Conventions users expect (check against the contract):** large, high-quality, optimised imagery; plans and sections viewable; credits.
**Pitfalls:** tiny thumbnails, generic "luxury living" copy, 3D models that block the page from loading.

**Worked example, brief to concept:** `examples/concepts/architecture.md`

## Recipe contract

| | |
|---|---|
| **Intended context** | practices, interior studios and property developers showing a portfolio of spaces |
| **Assumes** | a project-led practice with photography and drawings |
| **Does not cover** | a property listings portal (search, filters, map, saved homes, agent contact): research it as a marketplace |
| **Usually required capabilities** (confirm in the contract) | enquiry route, project index, project detail with credits |
| **Optional treatments** | walkthrough films, drawing/photo lens, archetype choices; none of them is required, and the concept may invent something no recipe names |
| **Responsive and accessibility** | the primary action stays one tap away at 375px; every treatment has a keyboard, touch and reduced-motion path |
| **Reject or adapt when** | the visitor needs to search inventory (listings) rather than admire projects; imagery is unavailable or poor |

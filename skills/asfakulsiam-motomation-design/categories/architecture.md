# Category: Architecture / Interiors / Real estate

**The real job:** space, light and material conveyed through the screen, then a clear way to enquire.
**Default mood:** Quiet · Dials ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2 (Tier 4–5 for a walkthrough)

**Archetypes that work:** Room · Atlas · Catalogue (projects as numbered entries) · Lookbook

**Section order:** hook (one space, full-bleed) → projects index → project pages (drawings + photos + facts: area, year, location, team) → studio/approach → enquire.
**Signature menu:** a Motomation walkthrough (image sequence from a render) · Lens between drawing and photo · Swiss grid lines that draw on load · Aperture transitions
**Must-haves:** large, high-quality, optimised imagery; plans and sections viewable; credits.
**Pitfalls:** tiny thumbnails, generic "luxury living" copy, 3D models that block the page from loading.

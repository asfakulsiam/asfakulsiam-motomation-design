# Category: Portfolio (designers, developers, photographers, artists)

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** be remembered for one thing in 30 seconds, then prove range for the people who stay.
**Starting dials** (the brief overrides; style is derived from the brief, with `styles/` as reference): ENERGY 3 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 3 → Tier 2 + one Motomation chapter

**Archetypes that can fit (optional):** Index · Specimen · Film Reel · Gallery Wall · Letter · Ledger

**One possible section order (adapt or replace):**
1. Hook: the name or a single statement, set as an image (the person, not "Hi, I'm…")
2. Selected work: 4–8 projects, each with a one-line outcome
3. A short-version summary per project (role, year, result) at the top of each case study
4. About: one paragraph with a point of view, plus a photo only if it adds something
5. Ask: one contact method, large
6. Sign-off: a giant name or a small game (an easter egg for the curious)

**Possible treatments (optional, at most one becomes a signature):** Letter Relay · Proximity Weight · Horizontal Reel · Lens on case images · Remembered List · Neighbour Moves

**Conventions users expect (check against the contract):** fast case-study loading, real project outcomes, keyboard-navigable work list, `llms.txt` and good meta for sharing.
**Pitfalls:** a bento grid of thumbnails, skill bars and percentages, a tools logo wall, "passionate about" copy.

**Award-site teardowns** (principles, no code): `references/teardowns/igloo-inc.md` · `references/teardowns/bruno-simon.md`

**Worked example, brief to concept:** `examples/concepts/portfolio.md`

## Recipe contract

| | |
|---|---|
| **Intended context** | designers, developers, photographers and artists showing work to be hired |
| **Assumes** | a curated set of projects with case studies |
| **Does not cover** | an agency with services, careers and many clients (see studio), or a stock/photo marketplace |
| **Usually required capabilities** (confirm in the contract) | work index, case study pages with outcomes, about, contact, a keyboard-navigable work list |
| **Optional treatments** | letter relays, horizontal reels, proximity type, film reels; none of them is required, and the concept may invent something no recipe names |
| **Responsive and accessibility** | the primary action stays one tap away at 375px; every treatment has a keyboard, touch and reduced-motion path |
| **Reject or adapt when** | the buyer is procurement (needs services, rates, process) rather than a creative director; work is mostly confidential |

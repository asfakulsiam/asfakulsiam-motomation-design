# Category: Organisation / Non-profit / Public sector

**The real job:** trust and clarity first: what you do, for whom, and how to act (donate, apply, volunteer, get help).
**Default mood:** Quiet or Editorial · Dials ENERGY 1–2 · RHYTHM 1 · MOTION 1–2

**Archetypes that work:** Manifesto · Timeline · Field Guide · Letter · Atlas (where you work)

**Section order:** mission in one sentence → the help or action people came for → evidence (real reports, real numbers with sources) → stories with consent → how to act → governance and contact.
**Signature menu:** Ink Fill for the mission · a Timeline of real milestones · Thread linking the problem to the action · a counter of verified figures (with a source link)
**Must-haves:** WCAG 2.2 AA throughout, plain language, multiple languages if the audience needs them (e.g. Bangla + English), low-bandwidth performance, transparent finances.
**Pitfalls:** guilt-driven imagery, unverifiable statistics, motion that harms people with vestibular disorders. Keep MOTION at 1–2.

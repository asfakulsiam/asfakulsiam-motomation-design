# Category: Organisation / Non-profit / Public sector

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** trust and clarity first: what you do, for whom, and how to act (donate, apply, volunteer, get help).
**Starting dials** (the brief overrides; style is derived from the brief, with `styles/` as reference): ENERGY 1–2 · RHYTHM 1 · MOTION 1–2

**Archetypes that can fit (optional):** Manifesto · Timeline · Field Guide · Letter · Atlas (where you work)

**One possible section order (adapt or replace):** mission in one sentence → the help or action people came for → evidence (real reports, real numbers with sources) → stories with consent → how to act → governance and contact.
**Possible treatments (optional, at most one becomes a signature):** Ink Fill for the mission · a Timeline of real milestones · Thread linking the problem to the action · a counter of verified figures (with a source link)
**Conventions users expect (check against the contract):** WCAG 2.2 AA throughout, plain language, multiple languages if the audience needs them (e.g. Bangla + English), low-bandwidth performance, transparent finances.
**Pitfalls:** guilt-driven imagery, unverifiable statistics, motion that harms people with vestibular disorders. Keep MOTION at 1–2.

**Award-site teardowns** (principles, no code): `references/teardowns/siena-film-foundation.md`

**Worked example, brief to concept:** `examples/concepts/organisation.md`

## Recipe contract

| | |
|---|---|
| **Intended context** | non-profits, foundations and public bodies that need trust and action |
| **Assumes** | a mission, programmes and clear calls to act |
| **Does not cover** | a government service (task-first, GOV.UK-style patterns) or a large institution with many departments: research them as services |
| **Usually required capabilities** (confirm in the contract) | the action people came for (donate, apply, get help) in one step from home, programmes, evidence, about and governance, accessibility statement, contact, language options where the audience needs them |
| **Optional treatments** | manifesto openings, milestone timelines, ink-fill missions; none of them is required, and the concept may invent something no recipe names |
| **Responsive and accessibility** | the primary action stays one tap away at 375px; every treatment has a keyboard, touch and reduced-motion path |
| **Reject or adapt when** | people arrive in crisis or need a task done; any narrative delay is harm |

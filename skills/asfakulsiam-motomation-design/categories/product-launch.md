# Category: Product launch / Hardware / Single product

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** a moment of desire, then the reasons to believe, then pre-order or buy.
**Starting dials** (the brief overrides; style is derived from the brief, with `styles/` as reference): ENERGY 3 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 3 → **Motomation home ground**

**Archetypes that can fit (optional):** Film Reel · Stage · Poster · Catalogue (specs)

**One possible section order (adapt or replace):** hook (the reveal) → Motomation chapter: the product explained in 3–6 scenes → key specs as type → comparison or choice → price and buy → FAQ.
**Possible treatments (optional, at most one becomes a signature):** an image-sequence product film · Timecode Rail · Aperture reveal · Decode on spec labels
**Conventions users expect (check against the contract):** the price and buy action reachable at every moment (sticky after the hook), real specs, the static storyboard fallback, preloading strategy for frames.
**Pitfalls:** 1,000% pins that trap people, frames that load after the user has scrolled past, specs only inside the animation.

**Award-site teardowns** (principles, no code): `references/teardowns/prometheus-fuels.md`

## Recipe contract

| | |
|---|---|
| **Intended context** | a single hero product or hardware launch |
| **Assumes** | one product with media to tell its story |
| **Does not cover** | a product range or a store with many SKUs: research as commerce |
| **Usually required capabilities** (confirm in the contract) | price and buy or pre-order reachable at every moment, specs, comparison where the range has variants, FAQ, shipping and returns |
| **Optional treatments** | image-sequence films, timecode rails, aperture reveals; none of them is required, and the concept may invent something no recipe names |
| **Responsive and accessibility** | the primary action stays one tap away at 375px; every treatment has a keyboard, touch and reduced-motion path |
| **Reject or adapt when** | the audience is comparing on specs; mobile users on weak connections; the story would trap people in pins |

# Category: Product launch / Hardware / Single product

**The real job:** a moment of desire, then the reasons to believe, then pre-order or buy.
**Default mood:** Play or Dark Luxe · Dials ENERGY 3 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 3 → **Motomation home ground**

**Archetypes that work:** Film Reel · Stage · Poster · Catalogue (specs)

**Section order:** hook (the reveal) → Motomation chapter: the product explained in 3–6 scenes → key specs as type → comparison or choice → price and buy → FAQ.
**Signature menu:** an image-sequence product film · Timecode Rail · Aperture reveal · Decode on spec labels
**Must-haves:** the price and buy action reachable at every moment (sticky after the hook), real specs, the static storyboard fallback, preloading strategy for frames.
**Pitfalls:** 1,000% pins that trap people, frames that load after the user has scrolled past, specs only inside the animation.

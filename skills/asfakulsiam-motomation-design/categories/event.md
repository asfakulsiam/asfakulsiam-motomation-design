# Category: Event / Festival / Conference

**The real job:** excitement, then the facts (date, place, line-up, tickets) in seconds.
**Default mood:** Play · Dials ENERGY 3 · RHYTHM 3 · MOTION 3

**Archetypes that work:** Poster · Stage · Timeline (schedule) · Index (line-up)

**Section order:** hook (the poster, moving) → date, place and tickets (visible in the first screen) → line-up / speakers → schedule → venue and travel → FAQ → tickets again.
**Signature menu:** a Velocity Skew line-up marquee · Width Sprint on the event name · a Motomation poster film · Sticker Scatter on the logo
**Must-haves:** add-to-calendar, ticket links that work on mobile, an accessible schedule table, `Event` structured data.
**Pitfalls:** the date hidden under the animation, countdown timers as the only content.

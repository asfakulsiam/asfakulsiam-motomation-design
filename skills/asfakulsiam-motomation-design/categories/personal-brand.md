# Category: Personal brand / Creator / Writer

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** a recognisable voice and a reason to follow, subscribe or hire.
**Starting dials** (the brief overrides; style is derived from the brief, with `styles/` as reference): ENERGY 2 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2

**Archetypes that can fit (optional):** Letter · Broadsheet · Index (writing list) · Specimen

**One possible section order (adapt or replace):** hook (a point of view in one line) → the work (latest pieces, talks, products) → about (a story, not a CV) → subscribe / contact → archive.
**Possible treatments (optional, at most one becomes a signature):** Letter archetype with a signature that draws on scroll · Remembered List for read articles · Brand Selection colour
**Conventions users expect (check against the contract):** fast reading pages, RSS, good typography for long-form, newsletter that works without JS.
**Pitfalls:** a "creator template" look with social icons as the hero.

**Award-site teardowns** (principles, no code): `references/teardowns/lando-norris.md`

**Worked example, brief to concept:** `examples/concepts/personal-brand.md`

## Recipe contract

| | |
|---|---|
| **Intended context** | writers, creators and speakers building an audience |
| **Assumes** | a body of work to read, watch or follow |
| **Does not cover** | a creator storefront or a membership platform: research as commerce or subscription |
| **Usually required capabilities** (confirm in the contract) | work index, reading or watching pages tuned for long-form, about, subscribe that works without JS, contact or booking |
| **Optional treatments** | letter archetypes, remembered lists, broadsheet treatments; none of them is required, and the concept may invent something no recipe names |
| **Responsive and accessibility** | the primary action stays one tap away at 375px; every treatment has a keyboard, touch and reduced-motion path |
| **Reject or adapt when** | the archive is large: search, tags and filtering matter more than a voice-led homepage |

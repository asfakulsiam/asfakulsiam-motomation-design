# Category: Studio / Agency

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** show taste and thinking, so the right clients self-select and the wrong ones leave.
**Default mood:** Editorial · Dials ENERGY 2 · RHYTHM 3 · MOTION 2–3

**Archetypes that work:** Ledger · Index · Manifesto · Broadsheet · Stage · Film Reel

**Section order:**
1. Hook: a position statement, not a list of services
2. Work: 3–6 case studies with the problem → idea → result
3. How we think: a manifesto or method, set as reading, not icons
4. Clients and collaborators as typography (no logo soup unless the logos are the proof)
5. People: real names and roles
6. Ask: brief form or email, with expected response time

**Signature menu:** Ink Fill manifesto · Stacked Deck case studies · Velocity Skew client marquee · Aperture into case studies
**Must-haves:** case-study pages with real process images, credits, year and outcome.
**Pitfalls:** "We craft digital experiences" copy, awards badges as the hero, three service icons.

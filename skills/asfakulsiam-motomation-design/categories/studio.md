# Category: Studio / Agency

> **Standards vs style:** where this category's job conflicts with house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent), the category wins; the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md` always apply.

**The real job:** show taste and thinking, so the right clients self-select and the wrong ones leave.
**Starting dials** (the brief overrides; style is derived from the brief, with `styles/` as reference): ENERGY 2 · RHYTHM 3 · MOTION 2–3

**Archetypes that can fit (optional):** Ledger · Index · Manifesto · Broadsheet · Stage · Film Reel

**One possible section order (adapt or replace):**
1. Hook: a position statement, not a list of services
2. Work: 3–6 case studies with the problem → idea → result
3. How we think: a manifesto or method, set as reading, not icons
4. Clients and collaborators as typography (no logo soup unless the logos are the proof)
5. People: real names and roles
6. Ask: brief form or email, with expected response time

**Possible treatments (optional, at most one becomes a signature):** Ink Fill manifesto · Stacked Deck case studies · Velocity Skew client marquee · Aperture into case studies
**Conventions users expect (check against the contract):** case-study pages with real process images, credits, year and outcome.
**Pitfalls:** "We craft digital experiences" copy, awards badges as the hero, three service icons.

**Award-site teardowns** (principles, no code): `references/teardowns/lusion-v3.md` · `references/teardowns/messenger.md` · `references/teardowns/active-theory.md`

## Recipe contract

| | |
|---|---|
| **Intended context** | creative studios and agencies selling services and culture |
| **Assumes** | a body of client work, services and a team |
| **Does not cover** | a production company with a large roster or a holding group with many brands |
| **Usually required capabilities** (confirm in the contract) | work index, case studies, services, about and team, careers, contact |
| **Optional treatments** | signature cursors, index archetypes, kinetic type; none of them is required, and the concept may invent something no recipe names |
| **Responsive and accessibility** | the primary action stays one tap away at 375px; every treatment has a keyboard, touch and reduced-motion path |
| **Reject or adapt when** | the studio sells to procurement-led clients who need capabilities, process and proof up front |

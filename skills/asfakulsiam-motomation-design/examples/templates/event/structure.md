- **What it is:** A one-night concert as a poster: the name under stage light, the lineup in running order, tickets drawn as tickets, and how to get to the hall.

## Sections, in order

### 1. hero (`hero-event-poster`)

A one-night jazz concert's page is a poster: stage light falling through a high window, the title as heavy type that swells under the cursor, and the date on a split-flap board.

- **Why it's memorable:** One screen holds everything a poster would (what, when, where, price); the stage light is the venue's real clerestory, and the flap board is the date, not a countdown gimmick.
- **Mobile:** Title stacks on three lines; the date board wraps under it; the light is the still poster on low-end phones.
- **Reduced motion:** Still light, final date, title at its resting weight.

### 2. team (`team-event-lineup`)

The night's lineup: a marquee of every musician's name that leans with scroll speed, then the four sets in running order, each row flooding with the players' instruments on hover.

- **Why it's memorable:** The running order is the information concert-goers want (who, when), and the marquee is the house's billboard; the people, not a "lineup" graphic.
- **Mobile:** The marquee keeps running at its base speed; sets are full-width rows that open on tap.
- **Reduced motion:** The marquee is a static line of names; rows invert without the flowing text.

### 3. pricing (`pricing-event-tickets`)

Three ticket tiers drawn as actual paper tickets (stub, perforation, seat block) that tilt toward the cursor like a ticket held to the light; the gallery tier's brass stamp catches the light once.

- **Why it's memorable:** The tickets are the objects you'll hold at the door, with the real seat blocks; one brass stamp, not three badges.
- **Mobile:** Tickets stack full width; no tilt; the stamp is still.
- **Reduced motion:** No tilt; solid stamp.

### 4. contact (`contact-event-venue`)

The night's practical details (venue, doors, getting there) with a small dock of the four things people do next: add to calendar, open the map, share, and call the box office.

- **Why it's memorable:** A dock of real next actions instead of a contact form; magnification makes the target you're reaching for bigger, which matters on a crowded footer.
- **Mobile:** The dock is a static row of large buttons.
- **Reduced motion:** No magnification; the buttons are the same size.

- **What it is:** Noor Haque designs title sequences in Dhaka. The reel on this page is a film you play by scrolling: four shots, four commissions.

## Chapters, in order

### 1. Hook (still, about one screen)

The title, a lead and the reel's meta. Nothing animates; the film's first frame is preloaded below.

### 2. Reel (pinned film, about five screens of scroll)

Scroll is the playhead. Four shots, one per commission, each with its caption beside the film (one caption on at a time). A running mm:ss:ff timecode, the current shot and a progress bar sit under the film: the signature.

- **Mobile:** its own portrait frame set; same four shots and captions.
- **Reduced motion:** the storyboard: four stills in normal flow, each with its caption; no canvas.

### 3. Credits (still)

The commissions as a credits list: a still page after the film.

### 4. Booking (still)

How to book: one action; its hover fills with the accent.

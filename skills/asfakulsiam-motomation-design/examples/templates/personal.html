Letters from Nusrat · No. 37 · October

Dear reader,Dear reader,

I've written to you once a month for three years now, mostly about the people who make things by hand in this city and what they told me while they worked.

This month: a bookbinder in Banglabazar who has bound every edition of one poet since 1974, and won't read any of them.

If you'd like the next letter, there's a box at the bottom of this one.

— Nusrat

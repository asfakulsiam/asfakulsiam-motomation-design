# Structure: River Commons · a public trust for Bangladesh's rivers

> Design reference, not code. Build this in the stack Step 0 chose (`thinking/sequence.md`); do not copy a file layout from
> anywhere into a different stack. Screenshot: `og.jpg`. Decisions (concept, type, colour, imagery, restraint): `DESIGN.md`.

- **Kind:** public-interest organisation
- **Archetype · mutation · signature:** manifesto · constraint · seismograph-line
- **Tier:** 2
- **Type roles:** display Gloock · text Schibsted Grotesk · mono JetBrains Mono
- **What it is:** A river trust's case in one page: the claim, the people who watch the banks, a year of hearings, gifts tied to real costs, and its papers.

## Sections, in order

### 1. hero (`hero-org-manifesto`)

A rivers charity opens with its argument, not its logo: one large sentence floats up word by word, then the paragraph that follows inks in as you read it.

- **Why it's memorable:** The Manifesto's speech rhythm: the visitor reads the whole case before seeing a donate button, which is why the button later converts.
- **Mobile:** The statement wraps to four or five lines at a smaller size; the paragraph is full width.
- **Reduced motion:** Statement and paragraph appear fully set.

### 2. team (`team-org-field-guide`)

The river watchers as field-guide entries (name, the reach each person watches, one line from their log) beside a single halftone survey print of the three rivers.

- **Why it's memorable:** The people who do the work, catalogued the way the trust catalogues rivers; the one halftone ties the page to the survey prints, and it shows the water, not the people.
- **Mobile:** The survey print sits above the entries; entries stack one per row.
- **Reduced motion:** The print is drawn at full size at once; nothing moves.

### 3. features (`features-org-impact`)

Three numbers from the trust's last year, set huge on a contour-line field like a survey map, each with the sentence that makes it mean something.

- **Why it's memorable:** The Manifesto backs its argument with evidence at the same scale as the words; the contour lines are the survey work itself.
- **Mobile:** Numbers stack, still large; contour lines stay behind at low contrast.
- **Reduced motion:** Lines are still; numbers show final values.

### 4. cta (`cta-org-donate`)

The donation ask states what each amount pays for (a watcher's month, a survey day, a court filing); choosing an amount sparks, and a completed gift gets one burst of confetti and a sentence naming what it bought.

- **Why it's memorable:** Amounts are tied to real costs from the audited budget; the celebration happens once, for the real success, and the sentence carries it.
- **Mobile:** Amounts are a two-column grid of large buttons.
- **Reduced motion:** No sparks or confetti; the confirmation sentence is the same.

### 5. footer (`footer-org-seismograph`)

The trust's footer sits under a single line that ripples where the pointer crosses it, like a river gauge's trace; then the registration details donors look for.

- **Why it's memorable:** The gauge line is the trust's instrument, and its only motion responds to the visitor; the registration numbers are what a careful donor checks last.
- **Mobile:** The line rests flat (no pointer); columns stack.
- **Reduced motion:** The line is flat and still.

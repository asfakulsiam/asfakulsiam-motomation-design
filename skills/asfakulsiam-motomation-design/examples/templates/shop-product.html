The record of No. 14-07

Four steps, five weeks.

  1. 01 Clay

    Week 1 · Bijoypur, Netrokona

    White clay dug from one hillside, washed twice and left to age in the shade for a week.

  2. 02 Throwing

    Week 2 · 22 plates a day

    Thrown by Rina Hajong on a kick wheel, trimmed when leather-hard, signed on the foot.

  3. 03 Glaze

    Week 3 · ash from our own kiln

    Wood ash and feldspar, sieved to 80 mesh; the green comes from iron in the ash.

  4. 04 Firing

    Week 5 · 30 hours, 1,280 °C

    Jackfruit wood, fed every seven minutes. About one plate in eight doesn't survive.

- **What it is:** A heritage hotel in Sonargaon kept like an old print: the courtyard develops beside the name, then the guest book, the rooms, the rates and the way from Dhaka.

## Sections, in order

### 1. hero (`hero-hotel-room`)

A small heritage hotel's first screen is split like a register page and a print: the name stacked at the top left, the house's line at the foot, and the courtyard photograph on the right developing from blank paper as you scroll.

- **Why it's memorable:** The courtyard appears the way an old photograph does in the tray, pale first, then tone; it is the only motion on the page, so it's remembered, and it says the house is old before any copy does.
- **Mobile:** The photograph comes first at full width (4:5), the stacked name below it; the develop still runs on scroll.
- **Reduced motion:** The photograph is fully developed from the start; nothing else on the page moves.

### 2. testimonial (`testimonial-hotel-guestbook`)

One entry from the guest book, typed out on grained paper as if written at the desk, with the room and the date.

- **Why it's memorable:** The hotel's real guest book replaces review stars; the typing is the guest writing, and it plays once.
- **Mobile:** The page is full width with a narrower measure.
- **Reduced motion:** The entry is complete; the grain is still.

### 3. features (`features-hotel-rooms`)

The rooms as the upstairs gallery's row of arched doors, held still: one large arch for the room most guests ask for, four smaller arches beside it, each photograph cut to the arch and captioned with the one thing only that room has.

- **Why it's memorable:** The arch mask is the house's own doorway shape; nothing moves here, so the courtyard developing above stays the page's only motion.
- **Mobile:** One column; the large arch first, the others at full width below it.
- **Reduced motion:** Nothing here moves.

### 4. pricing (`pricing-hotel-seasons`)

Rates by season as three dark cards lit by a pool of lamplight under the pointer, each with what the house is like then (not just the price).

- **Why it's memorable:** Seasons are the hotel's real price structure, and each card sells the room at that time of year; the lamplight is the courtyard at night.
- **Mobile:** Cards stack; the light rests at the top of each.
- **Reduced motion:** Fixed soft light at the top of each card.

### 5. contact (`contact-hotel-atlas`)

How to arrive, set as a survey sheet: the route from Dhaka in three legs, with the address and phone to copy for the driver.

- **Why it's memorable:** The Atlas archetype answers the guest's real question (how do I get there in Dhaka traffic), and the copy buttons hand the details to a driver.
- **Mobile:** Legs stack; the address and phone each keep their copy button.
- **Reduced motion:** Nothing here moves; the copy buttons confirm with text, not motion.

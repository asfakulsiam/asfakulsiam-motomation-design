- **Kind:** hotel / hospitality
- **Archetype · mutation · signature:** room · reverse-reveal · aperture
- **Tier:** 2
- **Type roles:** display Cormorant Garamond · text Instrument Sans · mono JetBrains Mono
- **What it is:** A heritage hotel that opens with a door: the courtyard, the upstairs gallery of rooms, rates by season, one guest-book entry, and the way from Dhaka.

## Sections, in order

### 1. hero (`hero-hotel-room`)

A small heritage hotel's first screen is a doorway: as you scroll, the arched door opens onto the courtyard, and the name sits on the threshold.

- **Why it's memorable:** You arrive the way guests do, through the door; the Room archetype's threshold is the only motion on the page, so it's remembered.
- **Mobile:** The doorway keeps its 16:9 opening at full width with the name below it; the scroll-driven opening still runs.
- **Reduced motion:** The doorway is fully open from the start.

### 2. features (`features-hotel-rooms`)

The seven rooms as a horizontal walk along the gallery: vertical scroll moves you sideways from door to door, each frame a room with its one detail.

- **Why it's memorable:** Walking past the rooms in order, the way the upstairs gallery runs, is the Room archetype's corridor; each frame names one thing only that room has.
- **Mobile:** Frames are 85% of the screen wide; the reel becomes a horizontal swipe.
- **Reduced motion:** Frames become a normal horizontal scroller (no scroll-jacking).

### 3. pricing (`pricing-hotel-seasons`)

Rates by season as three dark cards lit by a pool of lamplight under the pointer, each with what the house is like then (not just the price).

- **Why it's memorable:** Seasons are the hotel's real price structure, and each card sells the room at that time of year; the lamplight is the courtyard at night.
- **Mobile:** Cards stack; the light rests at the top of each.
- **Reduced motion:** Fixed soft light at the top of each card.

### 4. testimonial (`testimonial-hotel-guestbook`)

One entry from the guest book, typed out on grained paper as if written at the desk, with the room and the date.

- **Why it's memorable:** The hotel's real guest book replaces review stars; the typing is the guest writing, and it plays once.
- **Mobile:** The page is full width with a narrower measure.
- **Reduced motion:** The entry is complete; the grain is still.

### 5. contact (`contact-hotel-atlas`)

How to arrive, drawn as a survey sheet: the route from Dhaka in three legs over slow contour lines, with the address and phone to copy for the driver.

- **Why it's memorable:** The Atlas archetype answers the guest's real question (how do I get there in Dhaka traffic), and the copy buttons hand the details to a driver.
- **Mobile:** Legs stack; contour lines stay behind at low contrast.
- **Reduced motion:** Contour lines are still.

River Commons · a public trust for Bangladesh's rivers

A river is not a drain, a road, or a border. It is a neighbour, and it is owed a hearing.

Bangladesh has more than seven hundred rivers. Around a hundred have died in living memory, filled, fenced or poisoned. Since 2019 the courts have recognised rivers as living entities. We exist to make that ruling mean something on the bank: we survey, we go to court, and we pay local people to watch their water.

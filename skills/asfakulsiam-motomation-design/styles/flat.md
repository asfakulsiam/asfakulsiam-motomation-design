# Style: Flat / Illustrative

**Essence:** solid colour, clear shapes, friendly illustration, no depth effects.
**Use when:** education, public services, children's products, explainer-heavy products.
**Avoid when:** luxury or very technical audiences.

- **Type:** a friendly grotesk with open apertures (Onest, Hanken Grotesk, Instrument Sans).
- **Colour:** a limited palette of 3–5 flat colours with strong contrast pairs.
- **Layout:** illustration-led sections, clear steps, big tap targets.
- **Motion:** Tier 1–3. Lottie or Rive illustrations that explain, SVG line drawing (Thread).
- **Signature ideas:** a mascot or shape built in Rive that reacts to form states.
- **Pitfall:** stock "corporate blob people" illustrations. Commission or build a specific visual system.

# Style: Swiss / International

**Essence:** objective, grid-true, typographic clarity. Strong asymmetry, flush-left, rag-right.
**Use when:** precision is the brand: engineering, architecture, cultural institutions, systems products.
**Avoid when:** the brand's warmth or play matters more than clarity.

- **Type:** a neo-grotesk with character (Switzer, Neue Montreal, Schibsted Grotesk), with sizes on a strict modular scale.
- **Colour:** white or off-white, black, and one pure signal (red, orange, blue).
- **Layout:** a visible modular grid, numbers as structure, large type aligned to grid lines, flush-left everything.
- **Motion:** Tier 1–2. Elements slide along grid lines only (x or y, never diagonals), with hard ease-out.
- **Signature ideas:** a grid that draws itself on load, index numbers that count to their value, Ledger archetype.
- **Pitfall:** copying 1960s poster clichés (rotated type at 45° for no reason).

# Style: Organic / Natural

**Essence:** warmth, texture and imperfection: paper grain, soft curves, natural colour.
**Use when:** food, craft, wellness, sustainability, hospitality.
**Avoid when:** the brand is about speed or precision.

- **Type:** a serif with soft details (Fraunces with SOFT axis, Young Serif, Gambetta) and a humanist sans for text.
- **Colour:** earth and plant tones sampled from real photos. No neon.
- **Layout:** asymmetric, image-led, occasional hand-drawn marks or torn edges used with restraint.
- **Motion:** Tier 2. Slow (800–1200ms) drifts, Darkroom Develop, gentle parallax on texture layers.
- **Signature ideas:** text that "grows" (line reveal with a slight rotation settle), a seed-to-plant scroll film.
- **Pitfall:** rustic clichés (wood textures, script fonts everywhere).

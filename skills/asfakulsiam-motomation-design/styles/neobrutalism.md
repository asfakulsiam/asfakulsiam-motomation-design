# Style: Neobrutalism

**Essence:** honest, raw and loud: hard borders, flat colour, visible structure, a sense of humour.
**Use when:** youth brands, creative tools, indie products, events, anything that wants to be disarming.
**Avoid when:** luxury, healthcare, finance trust contexts.

- **Type:** a heavy grotesk or a quirky display (Bricolage Grotesque, Cabinet Grotesk, Unbounded). A mono for labels.
- **Colour:** 2–3 flat saturated colours with black ink; offset solid shadows (`4px 4px 0 #000`).
- **Layout:** thick 2–3px borders, stacked blocks, deliberate misalignment, sticker-like badges.
- **Motion:** Tier 1–2. Snappy (120–200ms), offset-shadow presses (the element moves into its shadow), no soft easing.
- **Signature ideas:** Sticker Scatter, a press that "stamps", a marquee with velocity skew.
- **Pitfall:** ugly ≠ brutalist. Spacing, alignment and hierarchy still have to be immaculate.

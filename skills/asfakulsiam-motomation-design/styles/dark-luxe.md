# Style: Dark Luxe

**Essence:** a dark room, one light source, precious details.
**Use when:** premium products, hotels, fragrance, watches, automotive, night-life.
**Avoid when:** accessibility-critical public information, or bright, friendly brands.

- **Type:** a refined high-contrast serif (Bodoni Moda, Gloock, Zodiak) or a wide elegant sans, set with generous tracking at small sizes.
- **Colour:** near-black grounds (#0B0B0C–#151412), warm off-white ink, metallic accents (brass, champagne) used tiny.
- **Layout:** centred compositions are allowed here as a ritual, with lots of black space.
- **Motion:** Tier 2–5. Light sweeps, slow push-ins, product turntables via image sequence (Motomation).
- **Signature ideas:** a Motomation product film, Aperture transitions, a cursor torch that lights the scene.
- **Pitfall:** grey-on-black contrast failures and "gold gradient" clichés.

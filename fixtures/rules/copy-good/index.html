Twelve rooms on Shankhari Bazar

The rooms sit above three conch-shell workshops. You will hear the saws until six, then the lane goes quiet.

Rooms

Twelve rooms, four with a balcony over the lane. Rooms start at 4,800 taka a night with breakfast on the roof.

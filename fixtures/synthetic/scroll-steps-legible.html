Kiln, in three firings

A fixture for the Delivery Gate. Scroll the list; the current step is lit.

  1. Bisque

    The first firing turns clay into ceramic at about 1000 degrees, slowly, over a night.

  2. Glaze

    Glaze goes on by hand. Each pot is dipped once and wiped clean at the foot.

  3. Reduction

    The second firing starves the kiln of air, and the iron in the glaze turns green.

  4. Cooling

    Two days with the door shut. Opening early cracks the glaze.

End of the sequence.

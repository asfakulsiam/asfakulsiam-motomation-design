# Motomation Design rule (for tools that read plain rules files)

For any web design, redesign, animation or critique task, follow `skills/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/SKILL.md`.
Define the product contract first (`thinking/product-contract.md`), research and synthesise the direction (Thinking Sequence), set the three dials, choose the lowest motion tier that delivers the concept,
and pass the Delivery Gate before handing back. Never ship the AI-default look. Read `gates/rules.md` before designing,
research the exact product type and the design landscape (Steps 1–2, `craft/research.md`); opinions are advisory and recipes optional (`principles/authority.md`), and ship `DESIGN.md` with `SCHEMA.md` (`templates/SCHEMA.md`).

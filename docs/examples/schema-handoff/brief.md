# Brief (v2.7.0 schema demo)

A one-page site for **Daftari**, a two-person bookbinding workshop in Banglabazar, Dhaka. They repair old books
(rebacking, resewing, new covers) and bind new commissions (thesis copies, notebooks, family albums). Visitors should see
the three services with starting prices, how a job moves through the bench, and send a repair or commission request with
a photo. Opening hours Saturday to Thursday, 10:00–19:00. Stack: plain HTML/CSS/JS on a static host; the request form
posts to a small serverless function the backend developer will write.

Prices: rebacking from 900 taka, full resew from 1,800 taka, thesis binding from 450 taka a copy.

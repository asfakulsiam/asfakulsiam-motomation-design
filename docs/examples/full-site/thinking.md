## Thinking Sequence: Kheya Press (four pages)

1. **Tension:** a press that sells only by hand, one title at a time, 500 copies each ↔ a website that is open to everyone, all night, with no counter and no shopkeeper.
2. **Concept:** The site is the press's issue card: every book you open is stamped with today's date, so the site remembers your visit the way a library card remembers a reader.
3. **Archetype + mutation:** The Lookbook (full-bleed editorial spreads, page by page), mutated by a self-imposed constraint: one weight only. Every word on the site is EB Garamond 400 or Noto Serif Bengali 400; hierarchy comes from size, italic and space. Broken rule: a lookbook leans on photography and contrast of weight; this one has neither.
4. **Collision:** stamp and postmark (post) → steal "ink impression with a date". The date is real: the visitor's own date, never an invented one. The first title in the list is literally a post office.
5. **Signature move:** The Issue Stamp. On a book's page, a vermilion date stamp lands on its issue card once, with today's date ("OPENED · 11 OCT 2026"). On the Books page, any title you have opened keeps its small stamp (the Remembered List seed from Draw 3, folded into the same idea). One signature, two places, one meaning.
6. **Anti-Sameness Check:** memory has no earlier run in this project. AI-default scan: no hero → logos → features → testimonials; no centred headline with two buttons; no bento; the nav is a running head, not "logo left, links centre, button right"; one accent, one display face. Obvious version for a publisher: a shop grid of cover photos with "Add to cart". This plan differs in structure (spreads, a register), type (one weight), colour (paper and stamp ink only), motion (one stamp), signature and imagery (no images at all).
7. **Restraint Gate:** see the record in DESIGN.md. Concept survived: yes.

Dials: ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 1 → Tier 1. The books are quiet and the press sells by hand; the one moment is the stamp, which CSS and 40 lines of script can carry.
Type mode: HOUSE STYLE (a publisher's brand site: the visitor came for an impression of the list and its taste, and the Bangla titles are the strongest image the press has).

# Anti-Slop & Design Taste Layer

This skill inherits the strongest ideas from:

- **Hallmark** (Nutlope) — refuses generic AI design
- **Anti-slop** (miqdadbadjuber) — filter rules against AI-shaped UI and copy
- **Taste Skill** (Leonxlnx) — forces intentional layout, type, spacing, hierarchy, and motion

The goal is simple: **never ship something that looks or feels AI-generated.**

---

## Core Anti-Slop Rules (Always Active)

1. **Reject on-distribution defaults**  
   Inter, system fonts, purple gradients, generic card grids, fade-up-everything, and “modern SaaS” clichés are banned unless the brief explicitly demands them.

2. **Force a design decision**  
   Every page must have a clear visual point of view. “Clean and modern” is not a point of view.

3. **Liveliness over sterility**  
   The result should feel alive (through type, spacing, motion, or composition) rather than merely correct.

4. **No filler copy**  
   Never invent testimonials, metrics, feature lists, or project results.

5. **Purpose before polish**  
   Every animation, decoration, and layout choice must serve hierarchy, feedback, or storytelling.

6. **Variance is required**  
   Sections should not all use the same composition, entrance, or density.

---

## Three Design Dials (from Taste Skill spirit)

When designing, consciously set these three dials:

| Dial       | Low                          | High                              |
|------------|------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Variance** | Consistent, quiet system    | Strong section-to-section contrast |
| **Motion**   | Minimal, restrained         | Expressive, cinematic             |
| **Density**  | Generous whitespace         | Information-rich, tighter         |

Match the dials to the project personality and the current mood (Quiet / Editorial / Play).

---

## Hard Gates (Fail = Start Over)

- Does the design have a clear Signature Move?
- Would a designer immediately recognize this as generic AI output? If yes → reject.
- Is the typography distinctive and hierarchical?
- Does motion serve the story or is it just decoration?
- Is there a complete reduced-motion experience?

---

## Relationship to Motomation

Anti-slop rules apply at every Motomation tier.  
A Tier 5 experience that still looks like every other AI portfolio is a failure.  
Originality and restraint are more important than technical complexity.

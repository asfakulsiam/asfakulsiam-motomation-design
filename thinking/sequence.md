# How a Designer Thinks — Mandatory Sequence

Every new design request must pass through this sequence. Skipping any step produces generic output. State the answers before coding.

## 1. Tension

Find the productive contradiction inside the brief.

Examples:
- Luxury brand that must feel approachable
- Technical product that must feel human
- Minimal site that still needs to feel alive
- Portfolio that must feel expensive without looking corporate

Write the tension as a short phrase.

## 2. Concept

Translate the tension into a single guiding metaphor.

Examples:
- “The site is a precision instrument”
- “The site is a quiet gallery that occasionally comes alive”
- “The site is a living material that responds to the hand”
- “The site is a film the user directs by scrolling”

The metaphor must be specific enough to generate layout, type, and motion decisions.

## 3. Archetype + Mutation

Pick a known visual language, then deliberately break one of its rules.

Archetype examples: pure minimal, editorial, soft glass, hard neobrutal, monochrome luxury, industrial, organic.

Mutation examples:
- Minimal but with one aggressive scale jump
- Glassmorphism but with hard geometric frames instead of soft edges
- Editorial but with unexpected motion that breaks the grid
- Neobrutal but with refined typography instead of raw display faces

## 4. Forced Collision

Combine three elements that do not normally live together:

- One visual archetype
- One material or decorative treatment (grain, hard shadow, soft blur, monoline, heavy type, etc.)
- One motion behaviour (scroll scrub, magnetic cursor, staggered reveal, pinned timeline, 3D orbit, etc.)

The collision is the seed of originality.

## 5. Signature Move

Invent one interaction or compositional moment that appears only once on the entire site and becomes the thing people remember.

Examples:
- A single word that expands into a full paragraph on scroll
- A product image that turns into a 3D model only in one section
- A navigation that dissolves into the page content
- A hero that is pure type until the user moves, then reveals image

Never repeat the signature move.

## 6. Anti-Sameness Check

List three common AI / template defaults you will refuse on this project.

Typical banned defaults:
- Inter / system-ui + soft blue accent
- Card grid with equal padding and soft shadows
- Fade-up on every section
- Generic hero with left text / right image
- Overuse of backdrop-blur without purpose

Confirm the resulting design would not be confused with last week’s output.

## 7. Restraint Gate

For every visual flourish or animation proposed later, ask:

Does this serve hierarchy, state feedback, or storytelling?

If the answer is only “it looks cool”, delete it.

Most ideas should die here. The surviving ones become powerful.

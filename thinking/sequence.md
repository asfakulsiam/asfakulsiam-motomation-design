# Thinking Sequence — Full Guide

This is the expanded version of the mandatory 7-step sequence.

## 1. Tension
Identify the core contradiction in the brief.  
Examples: luxury yet approachable, minimal yet expressive, playful yet serious, technical yet human.

## 2. Concept
Write one clear sentence that acts as the metaphor for the entire site.  
Examples: “the site is a quiet gallery”, “the site is a precision instrument”, “the site is a living material”.

## 3. Archetype + Mutation
Choose a recognizable base visual language, then force one deliberate mutation that makes it unique to this project.

## 4. Forced Collision
Combine three unrelated elements:
- one archetype
- one material or decoration
- one motion behaviour

This forces originality.

## 5. Signature Move
Invent one memorable interaction or compositional moment that appears only once on the site and defines its personality.  
It must be specific and describable.

## 6. Anti-Sameness Check
Explicitly list three common AI defaults you will avoid.  
Confirm the design would not look like last week’s generic output.

## 7. Restraint Gate
For every proposed animation or visual flourish, answer:  
“Does this serve hierarchy, feedback, or storytelling?”  
If the answer is no, delete it.

---

Only after completing all seven steps may implementation begin.

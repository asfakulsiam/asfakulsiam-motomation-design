# Commands

These directional commands can be issued at any time. Apply only the requested change and, if the concept shifts, restate the relevant part of the thinking sequence.

## /shape

Restructure the overall composition, hierarchy, and spatial relationships. Do not change color or motion language unless required by the new structure.

## /bolder

Increase visual impact: larger type, stronger contrast, more decisive scale relationships, more confident use of space or color.

## /quieter

Reduce noise. Increase breathing room. Soften or remove secondary elements. Let the primary content dominate more completely.

## /distill

Remove everything that is not essential to the core idea. Apply the restraint gate aggressively.

## /animate

Introduce or refine motion at the current tier. Improve easing, timing, stagger, and purposefulness. Do not escalate the tier unless asked.

## /motomate

Escalate the experience to tier 5. Redesign key sequences so the scroll feels like a directed film. Preserve the core concept and signature move.

## /mutate

Force a new original pattern using the invention layer. Generate a fresh collision or signature move and rebuild around it.

## /overdrive

Push motion intensity and visual drama to the maximum that still passes the restraint gate. The result should feel intense but intentional, never chaotic.

## /critique

Run a strict review against principles, restraint, anti-sameness, accessibility, and performance. List concrete problems and proposed fixes. Do not rewrite the whole design unless asked.

## /polish

Refine micro-details: type spacing, easing curves, hover states, focus styles, small timing adjustments, optical alignment. Do not change the big idea.

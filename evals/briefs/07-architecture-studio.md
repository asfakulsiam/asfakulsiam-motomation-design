# Brief 07: Architecture studio (benchmark: studio portfolio)
Website for **Ittaghor Studio**, a three-partner architecture practice in Dhaka known for brick and courtyard houses. Partners: Nusrat Jahan, Tanvir Ahmed, Farhan Kabir. Founded 2011.

## Content
- Positioning: "Houses built around light, brick and an open courtyard."
- Five featured projects (name · place · year · one line):
  1. Courtyard House · Bashundhara, Dhaka · 2019 · A family home wrapped around a single neem tree.
  2. Jali House · Rajshahi · 2021 · Perforated brick screens that cool the rooms by shading them.
  3. River Room · Sirajganj · 2022 · A weekend house raised above the monsoon line.
  4. Brick Library · Khulna · 2023 · A school library lit only from above.
  5. Twin Courts · Chattogram · 2024 · Two households, two courtyards, one shared wall.
- Each project shows a simple plan (draw it in SVG) and an "image" (CSS or SVG).
- Studio, process (site visit → sketch → brick sample → build) and contact: studio@ittaghor.studio.

**Expect:** Quiet mood, large imagery, plans viewable, a signature tied to light, brick or courtyards.

## Benchmark conditions (identical for baseline and skill runs)
- Deliver **one self-contained `index.html`** (inline CSS and JS). Allowed external resources: Google Fonts or Fontshare CSS, and GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger from a CDN. No other libraries, no image URLs: draw imagery with CSS, SVG or canvas.
- Use only the facts in this brief. Don't invent clients, logos, testimonials or statistics; leave them out.
- It must work at 375px and 1440px wide, by keyboard, and with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

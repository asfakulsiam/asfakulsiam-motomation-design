# "Ghat" · a short film pitch
Tier: 5 — Motomation
Why this tier: the pitch is the film, so scroll must be the playhead (a pinned canvas scrubbing frames, with a timecode rail) to let a funder feel the film's pace and images; anything lower would describe the film instead of showing it.

## The client
Director Sadia Islam and producer Mahmud Hasan are raising the last part of the budget for *Ghat*, a 14-minute fiction short set over one night at a river ferry terminal in Barishal. The site is sent privately to funders, festivals' development programmes and co-producers. It replaces a PDF deck: the reader should experience the film's rhythm, then get the facts and the ask.

## What they need
- Tell the story as one continuous scroll where scroll drives time: the reader moves the playhead through the film's night.
- Show where they are in the film at every moment (a timecode rail and the current scene).
- After the film, give funders the facts they need: team, budget, schedule, what the money buys.
- End on one clear ask: request the full pitch pack or a call.

## Pages
- One page, no breaks: an opening title, the scroll film (pinned canvas, scrubbed frames, timecode rail), then the pitch facts and the ask, in one continuous flow.

## Content
- Title: *Ghat*. Logline: "On the last ferry night before the terminal closes, a ticket clerk has until dawn to decide whether to leave with the river."
- Film: 14 minutes, Bangla with English subtitles, shooting planned for January 2027 in Barishal.
- The scroll film follows six scenes (timecodes are the film's, for the rail):
  1. 00:00 "Last call" — the terminal at dusk, loudspeakers, crowds thinning.
  2. 02:10 "Ticket window" — Rina (the clerk) counts the last tickets by lamplight.
  3. 04:45 "The launch" — the final ferry's lights on the water; a stranger misses it.
  4. 07:30 "Night shift" — Rina and the stranger wait on the empty pontoon.
  5. 10:20 "Fog" — the river disappears; only horns.
  6. 12:40 "Dawn" — the terminal gates closing; Rina's choice.
- Frames: the director's storyboard frames, one sequence per scene, supplied later as image sequences; until then the site renders the sequence itself (drawn, generated or placeholder frames that clearly read as the scene), and documents where the real frames go.
- Team: Sadia Islam (writer-director; previous short *Dhulo* screened at two regional festivals), Mahmud Hasan (producer), Ayesha Karim (cinematographer).
- Budget: ৳ 38,00,000 total; ৳ 26,00,000 raised; ask: ৳ 12,00,000 for the shoot and post-production.
- Schedule: prep Nov–Dec 2026, shoot 10 days in January 2027, post Feb–Apr 2027, festival submissions from May 2027.
- Ask: "Request the pitch pack" → mailto:ghat.film@example.com ; "Book a call" → https://cal.example.com/ghat
- Languages: English text; scene titles also in Bangla where the director supplies them (optional).

## Constraints
- Tier 5: one pinned canvas, scrubbed frame sequences driven by scroll, and a timecode rail (current time, current scene) that stays visible while the film is pinned. Scroll is the playhead: scrolling back plays it back.
- Reduced-motion version: a static editorial sequence (each scene as a designed still with its title, timecode and a line of description, in normal document flow), not frozen frames of the canvas and not a blank pinned area.
- Mobile: a version composed for portrait screens, with the same scenes and rail.
- Performance: the opening title is readable before the frames load; frames load progressively; scrubbing stays smooth on a mid-range laptop.
- Accessible: the story is fully available as text; the rail is readable by screen readers; keyboard scrolling works; contrast AA over every frame.
- Stack: not specified by the client. Chosen at Step 0.

## Success
- A funder scrolls once through the night of the film and feels its pace before reading a single budget line.
- At any point they can tell which scene and minute they are in.
- With reduced motion, they get a beautiful, complete editorial sequence of the same six scenes, not a broken or frozen film.
- The ask is impossible to miss and easy to act on.

# Diversity brief 02: Children's dentistry clinic
Website for Little Molars, a children's dentistry clinic in Uttara, Dhaka, for ages 1 to 14. Two dentists, Dr. Nusrat Jahan and Dr. Arif Hossain [NEEDS COPY: credentials]. Parents book; children are the ones who are scared. The clinic wants first visits to feel like a game: the "tooth passport" (a stamp card for every visit), a waiting room with a fish tank, a gentle "tell-show-do" method. Services: first check-up, cleaning, fluoride, sealants, fillings, emergency for knocked-out teeth. Parents must be able to book a slot online (name, child's age, phone, preferred day). Pages: home, first visit, treatments, book.
Category: organisation (children's clinic).  Seed: 9202.
Approve every checkpoint.
**Expect:** a booking form with every state, playful but legible type, nothing that looks like a hospital.

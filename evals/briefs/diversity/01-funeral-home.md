# Diversity brief 01: Funeral home
Website for Shanti Ghat Funeral Service, a family-run funeral home in Narayanganj since 1974, serving Muslim, Hindu and Christian families. Families arrive in the worst week of their lives, often on a phone at night. They need to know three things fast: who to call right now (24-hour line 01711-000000 [NEEDS COPY: real number]), what happens next, and what it costs. Services: collection from home or hospital, washing and shrouding (ghusl / snan), cremation arrangements at the ghat, burial coordination, transport to the home district. Prices are fixed and published. Tone: quiet, plain, never salesy. Pages: home, what happens next, prices, contact.
Category: organisation (funeral home).  Seed: 9101.
Approve every checkpoint.
**Expect:** the phone number findable in one second at 375px, no stock photos of lilies, calm motion or none.

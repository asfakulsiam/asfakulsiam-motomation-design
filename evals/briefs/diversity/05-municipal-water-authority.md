# Diversity brief 05: Municipal water authority
Website for the Rajshahi City Water Supply Authority (public utility, 1.2 million residents). Residents come for four jobs: check whether their ward has a supply cut today, pay or understand a bill, report a leak or dirty water, and read the water quality report. The authority publishes a daily outage list by ward (live data), tariffs per 1,000 litres [NEEDS COPY: current tariff], and a monthly quality report (chlorine, turbidity, E. coli). Must work on old phones and slow 3G, in Bangla and English [NEEDS COPY: Bangla text]. Pages: home, outages, bills, report a problem, water quality.
Category: organisation (public utility).  Seed: 9505.
Approve every checkpoint.
**Expect:** today's outages first, a report form with every state, no decoration that costs bytes, accessibility beyond AA where cheap.

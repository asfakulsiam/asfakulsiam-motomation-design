# Nusrat Jahan: illustrator portfolio
Type: portfolio
Status: evaluation brief (v6). Fictional client; do not invent statistics, logos or testimonials.

## The client
Nusrat Jahan (fictional) is a book and editorial illustrator. Art directors visit to judge style range and to commission her.

## Product contract (expected)
Jobs:
- An art director sees range quickly, opens a project, and sends a commission enquiry with budget and deadline.

Core journeys (route sequences):
- Home → work index → project → next project
- Home → about → commission enquiry → confirmation

Required routes: Home, work index (filter by book, editorial, packaging), project detail pages, about with clients list (only the ones given), commission enquiry form, shop link (external), privacy, 404.

Capability levels: Enquiry form is frontend with validation and success state; sending is backend and may be deferred with the form kept.

## Pressures
- The work must lead; the site's own design must not compete with the illustrations.
- Many images vs fast loading.

## Bias probe
A biased skill would produce: a loud typographic concept that buries the illustrations; a Tier 5 film that hides the work index.

## Pass criteria
- Every required route exists and every core journey completes at 375 px and 1440 px (axis J, screenshots of each step).
- No control looks working without working; deferred capabilities keep their interface and are labelled (axis X).
- The art direction can be traced to the brief and the research record, not to a drawn archetype alone.
- Visual quality is judged only after the product-integrity gate passes (`evals/rubric.md`).

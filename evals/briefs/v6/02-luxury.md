# Meridian & Vale: independent watchmaker
Type: luxury
Status: evaluation brief (v6). Fictional client; do not invent statistics, logos or testimonials.

## The client
Meridian & Vale (fictional) makes about 300 mechanical watches a year in three collections. Buyers spend weeks deciding and usually book a private viewing.

## Product contract (expected)
Jobs:
- A collector understands each collection and its movement, then books a viewing or enquires about a reference.

Core journeys (route sequences):
- Home → collection → watch reference → book a viewing → confirmation
- Home → atelier (craft story) → enquiry

Required routes: Home, three collection pages, watch reference pages, atelier, book a viewing (date, boutique, reference), enquiry form, boutiques, care and service, privacy, terms, 404.

Capability levels: Viewing booking is a frontend form with validation and a success state; the booking itself is backend and may be deferred with the form kept.

## Pressures
- Luxury restraint vs the need to show technical specifications clearly (calibre, power reserve, case size).
- Slow, heavy imagery vs a 375 px phone on hotel Wi-Fi.

## Bias probe
A biased skill would produce: exuberant motion and a mandatory 'signature move' that slows the specification tables; dropping the booking form because 'luxury sites are minimal'.

## Pass criteria
- Every required route exists and every core journey completes at 375 px and 1440 px (axis J, screenshots of each step).
- No control looks working without working; deferred capabilities keep their interface and are labelled (axis X).
- The art direction can be traced to the brief and the research record, not to a drawn archetype alone.
- Visual quality is judged only after the product-integrity gate passes (`evals/rubric.md`).

# Fix It Dhaka: repair café and repairer directory
Type: community-directory
Status: evaluation brief (v6). Fictional client; do not invent statistics, logos or testimonials.

## The client
Fix It Dhaka (fictional) lists independent repairers (phones, fans, bicycles, clothes) and monthly repair cafés.

## Product contract (expected)
Jobs:
- A resident finds a repairer near them for a type of repair, sees hours and contact, and finds the next repair café.
- A repairer submits a listing.

Core journeys (route sequences):
- Home → search by repair type and area → results → repairer → call or directions
- Home → events → event detail → add to calendar
- Home → submit a listing → form → confirmation

Required routes: Home, directory with search and filters, results (list and map toggle), repairer profile, events list and detail, submit a listing, how it works, volunteer, about, contact, privacy, terms, 404.

Capability levels: Search and filters are frontend interaction over shipped data; submissions persisted in localStorage; moderation is backend and may be deferred with its interface kept.

## Pressures
- Bilingual (Bangla/English) labels and long Bangla words at 375 px.
- Community warmth vs utility of a directory.

## Bias probe
A biased skill would produce: a portfolio-style grid of hand-picked repairers instead of a searchable directory; dropping the submission flow.

## Pass criteria
- Every required route exists and every core journey completes at 375 px and 1440 px (axis J, screenshots of each step).
- No control looks working without working; deferred capabilities keep their interface and are labelled (axis X).
- The art direction can be traced to the brief and the research record, not to a drawn archetype alone.
- Visual quality is judged only after the product-integrity gate passes (`evals/rubric.md`).

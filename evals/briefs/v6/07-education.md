# CarePath: online courses for nurses
Type: education
Status: evaluation brief (v6). Fictional client; do not invent statistics, logos or testimonials.

## The client
CarePath (fictional) offers short accredited courses for working nurses, who study on phones during breaks.

## Product contract (expected)
Jobs:
- A nurse finds a course, checks accreditation and time needed, enrols, and resumes a lesson where they left off.

Core journeys (route sequences):
- Home → course catalogue → course → enrol → lesson 1
- My learning → resume lesson → quiz → result
- Home → accreditation → FAQ

Required routes: Home, course catalogue (filter by topic and hours), course detail with syllabus, enrol flow, my learning (progress), lesson page, quiz with result, accreditation, FAQ, help, privacy, terms, accessibility statement, 404.

Capability levels: Progress and quiz answers persisted in localStorage; accounts and certificates are backend and may be deferred with their interface kept.

## Pressures
- Short sessions on small screens vs depth of content.
- Trust and clarity vs a brand that wants to feel modern.

## Bias probe
A biased skill would produce: a landing page only; motion that slows lesson pages; low-contrast 'elegant' type for clinical content.

## Pass criteria
- Every required route exists and every core journey completes at 375 px and 1440 px (axis J, screenshots of each step).
- No control looks working without working; deferred capabilities keep their interface and are labelled (axis X).
- The art direction can be traced to the brief and the research record, not to a drawn archetype alone.
- Visual quality is judged only after the product-integrity gate passes (`evals/rubric.md`).

# Cargoline: freight visibility software
Type: b2b
Status: evaluation brief (v6). Fictional client; do not invent statistics, logos or testimonials.

## The client
Cargoline (fictional) sells shipment-tracking software to mid-size importers. Buyers are operations managers who compare vendors and must justify the purchase to finance and IT.

## Product contract (expected)
Jobs:
- An operations manager understands what the product does, sees it working, checks security and integrations, and books a demo or starts a trial.

Core journeys (route sequences):
- Home → product → integrations → pricing → book demo → confirmation
- Home → security → download security overview
- Home → customer stories → story → contact sales

Required routes: Home, product overview, three feature pages, integrations directory (filterable), pricing with plan comparison, security and compliance, customer stories index and detail, docs entry link, book a demo, contact sales, careers, privacy, terms, cookie policy, 404.

Capability levels: Demo booking and contact forms are frontend with validation; CRM submission is an integration and may be deferred with the form kept. Pricing toggle (monthly/yearly) is frontend interaction.

## Pressures
- Brand wants to look unlike every SaaS site; buyers expect conventional navigation, a pricing page and a comparison table.
- Legal requires no invented customer logos or metrics.

## Bias probe
A biased skill would produce: removing conventional navigation or the pricing table to look original; invented logo walls and statistics; a poster-like home page with no product screenshots.

## Pass criteria
- Every required route exists and every core journey completes at 375 px and 1440 px (axis J, screenshots of each step).
- No control looks working without working; deferred capabilities keep their interface and are labelled (axis X).
- The art direction can be traced to the brief and the research record, not to a drawn archetype alone.
- Visual quality is judged only after the product-integrity gate passes (`evals/rubric.md`).

# Leaf & Ledger, direction B: loud poster
Type: paired
Status: evaluation brief (v6). Fictional client; do not invent statistics, logos or testimonials.

## The client
Leaf & Ledger (fictional) sells single-estate teas from Bangladesh, India and Nepal by subscription and one-off order.

## Art direction (given by the client)
Art direction B: a loud, colourful poster direction (big type, saturated colour per origin, playful motion, Tier 3).

## Product contract (expected)
Jobs:
- A visitor chooses a tea or a subscription plan, buys it, and manages the subscription later.

Core journeys (route sequences):
- Home → shop → tea → add to cart → checkout → confirmation
- Home → subscriptions → choose plan → choose teas → checkout → confirmation
- Account → subscription → pause or skip → saved

Required routes: Home, shop with filters (origin, type, caffeine), tea detail, subscriptions and plan builder, cart, checkout, confirmation, account and subscription management, estates (origin stories), brewing guide, shipping, returns, privacy, terms, contact, 404.

Capability levels: Cart, plan builder and subscription changes are frontend interaction persisted in localStorage; payments are backend and may be deferred with their interface kept.

## Pressures
- Same product, two very different art directions: both must keep every route, journey and state above.
- The direction is given; the product contract is identical to the paired brief.

## Bias probe
A biased skill would produce: letting big type and motion push product grids, prices or the plan builder below several screens of spectacle.

## Pass criteria
- Every required route exists and every core journey completes at 375 px and 1440 px (axis J, screenshots of each step).
- No control looks working without working; deferred capabilities keep their interface and are labelled (axis X).
- The art direction can be traced to the brief and the research record, not to a drawn archetype alone.
- Visual quality is judged only after the product-integrity gate passes (`evals/rubric.md`).

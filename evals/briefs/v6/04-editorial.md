# The Delta Ledger: regional news publication
Type: editorial
Status: evaluation brief (v6). Fictional client; do not invent statistics, logos or testimonials.

## The client
The Delta Ledger (fictional) publishes daily news and long features about the Bengal delta, in English and Bangla, funded by members.

## Product contract (expected)
Jobs:
- Readers scan today's news, read articles comfortably on phones, follow topics and become members.

Core journeys (route sequences):
- Home → section → article → related article
- Home → search → results → article
- Article → membership → plan → sign-up form → confirmation

Required routes: Home (front page with many stories), sections (politics, climate, business, culture), article template, long-form feature template, author page, topic page, search with results, membership and plans, newsletter sign-up, corrections policy, about, contact, privacy, terms, 404.

Capability levels: Search over the shipped articles is frontend interaction; membership payment is backend and may be deferred with its form kept.

## Pressures
- Front page needs density and clear hierarchy across 20+ stories; the brand wants a distinctive typographic identity.
- Bangla and English must both render well.

## Bias probe
A biased skill would produce: a single-story magazine layout with one focal point per screen that cannot hold a front page; dropping search or the section pages.

## Pass criteria
- Every required route exists and every core journey completes at 375 px and 1440 px (axis J, screenshots of each step).
- No control looks working without working; deferred capabilities keep their interface and are labelled (axis X).
- The art direction can be traced to the brief and the research record, not to a drawn archetype alone.
- Visual quality is judged only after the product-integrity gate passes (`evals/rubric.md`).

The firing

How a cup gets its marks

Scroll slowly. The distance you travel is the time it takes: three parts stacking, one and a half firing, four cooling.

StackingFiringCooling
3days

Stacking

Three days to stack the kiln. A firing holds 20–40 pieces, so every cup, plate and vase has to find its place before anything is lit.

36hours

Firing

Mango wood feeds the fire for 36 hours. The ash glaze and the flame decide the rest, and no two cups come out marked the same way.

4days

Cooling

Four days of cooling while the glaze settles. The next firing is on 18 November, with 32 pieces.

The rest of the shelf

Everything comes out of a small firing of 20–40 pieces.

Mati fires 20–40 pieces at a time. This is how a batch gets to the shelf, starting from the end.

    of cooling

  2. 36 hours

    of firing with mango wood

    The flame and the ash decide how each piece is marked, which is why no two Firing Cups match.

  3. 3 days

    of stacking

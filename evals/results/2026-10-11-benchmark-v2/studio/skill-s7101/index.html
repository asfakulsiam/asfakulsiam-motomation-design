Four steps

Process

  1. Site visit

    Stand on the plot and see where the light comes from.

  2. Sketch

    Court, walls and rooms drawn together.

  3. Brick sample

    Brick and bond tried against real daylight.

  4. Build

    The house goes up, brick by brick, around its court.

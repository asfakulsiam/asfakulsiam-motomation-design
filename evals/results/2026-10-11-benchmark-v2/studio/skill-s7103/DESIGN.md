# DESIGN — Ittaghor Studio

Tension: brick is heavy and permanent ↔ what the practice actually builds is light, which moves all day.
Concept: A site where you move the sun, and every brick image and every plan shows where the light falls.
Archetype: The Specimen (type-led, one object shown at huge scale) mutated by "abuse the scale": project names and courtyards set 10x larger than a portfolio would.
Collision: boarding pass → the perforated tear line between each image and its plan (image is the ticket, plan is the stub).
Signature: sun-slider — one range control moves a beam of light across all images and a lit patch across all plans.
Dials: ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 1 → Tier 2 (GSAP intro tween + two ScrollTrigger draws).
Type: Instrument Serif (display, quiet and tall) / Instrument Sans (text). Type leads the first screen, imagery leads each project.
Palette: ground #F2ECE3, surface #E6DDD0, ink #241C17, muted #5E534A, accent brick #9A3F28; light ochre only inside imagery.
Not doing: full-bleed photo hero with centred title and a masonry grid of thumbnails.

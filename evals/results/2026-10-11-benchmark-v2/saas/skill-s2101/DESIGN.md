# DESIGN — Tracewell

## Plan
- Tension: 4 million lines of noise ↔ the one line an on-call engineer needs at 3 a.m.
- Concept: The page starts as a wall of log noise; one amber line survives, and a thread follows it down the page from ingest to explanation to share.
- Archetype: Console, mutated by "shift the tempo" (the hero's noise wall moves at film speed, everything below stays still).
- Collision: nakshi kantha → a running stitch telling a story: the thread rail stitches the three steps together.
- Signature: Thread — the surviving amber line becomes the rail that draws through the page. Echoed in the terminal result and the result view.
- Dials: ENERGY 2 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2 → Tier 2
- Type: Instrument Serif (display, one-line headlines, human voice against machine output) / Geist (text) / Geist Mono (commands, logs). Data-and-UI brief: type leads by clarity; display only in hero and section heads.
- Palette: paper #EFECE4, ink #15130F, muted #5B574D, terminal #14130F, signal #B23C07 on paper / #FF8A3D on terminal. Accent only on the one line.
- House-style override: light paper canvas with dark console panels (product UI is the proof); no giant display forced beyond the hero.
- Not doing: hero + logo wall + 3 icon features + testimonials; purple gradient; fake dashboard numbers.

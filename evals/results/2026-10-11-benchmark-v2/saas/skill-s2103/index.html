From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell is a CLI and web app that finds the one log line that explains an incident. Made for the engineer who is on call.

brew install tracewell

01 · How it works

Three steps. The second one does the finding.

  1. step 1

    tracewell ingest

    Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. step 2

    tracewell why --since 15m

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. step 3

    Share the link

    Send the explanation link to the team, so everyone starts from the same line.

Ingest, ask why, share the link.

  1. (01) tracewell ingest

    Streams logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. (02) tracewell why --since 15m

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. (03) Share the explanation link

    Send it to the team. They open the same line, with the reason.

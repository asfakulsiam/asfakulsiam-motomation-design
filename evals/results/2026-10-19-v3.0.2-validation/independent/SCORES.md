## Method and interpretation

I applied the four gates before computing weighted totals, using Concept 20%, Originality 20%, Typography 15%, Motion 15%, Craft 15%, and Access & performance 15%; the v5-only imagery exception was not applied to these v6 briefs. ([rubric](../../../rubric.md), [B2B brief](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.1-runs/briefs-used/03-b2b.md), [travel brief](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.1-runs/briefs-used/08-travel-booking.md), [12a brief](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.1-runs/briefs-used/12a-paired-archival.md), [12b brief](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.1-runs/briefs-used/12b-paired-poster.md))

I looked at the supplied 375/1440 homepage, inner-page, focus and reduced-motion images with the image-reading tool, then used a single Chromium browser at a time for interactions, request observation, route checks and new screenshots. ([supplied screenshots](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.2-scoring/scoring/), [browser test implementation](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.2-scoring/independent-v3.0.2/journeys.cjs), [additional checks](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.2-scoring/independent-v3.0.2/extra-checks.cjs))

The route audit opened 20 B2B, 24 travel, 23 archival and 23 poster addresses, including their explicit 404 pages, and measured document width at both 320 and 375 px. ([route/reflow audit](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.2-scoring/independent-v3.0.2/initial-browser-audit.json))

“Tab/Enter/Space-only” below means those keys for navigation/activation, with printable characters used solely to enter required text: no mouse, programmatic focus or arrow keys in the travel and tea primary checkout journeys. ([journey implementation](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.2-scoring/independent-v3.0.2/journeys.cjs), [completed primary journeys](https://github.com/asfakulsiam/asfakulsiam-motomation-design/blob/benchmarks/v3.0.2-scoring/independent-v3.0.2/journey-followup-evidence.json))

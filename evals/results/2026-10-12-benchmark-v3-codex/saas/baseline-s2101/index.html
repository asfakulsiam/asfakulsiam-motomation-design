How it works

One incident loop, shown end to end.

The page follows the same path the product does: ingest a live stream, ask why for the last 15 minutes, then hand the explanation over without dragging the team through raw logs.

01

Bring every source into one stream.

tracewell ingest

Use files, Docker or Kubernetes as the input.

02

Ask which line is genuinely unusual.

tracewell why --since 15m

Tracewell ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

03

Share the explanation, not the noise.

explanation link

Hand the result to the rest of the team with one link.

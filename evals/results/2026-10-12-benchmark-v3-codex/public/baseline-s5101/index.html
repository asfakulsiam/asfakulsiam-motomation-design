ম্যানগ্রোভ ফিরিয়ে আনিBringing the mangroves back

Sundari Trust restores mangroves in the Sundarbans buffer zone with local communities. The work starts in community nurseries run by women’s groups in Shyamnagar and Koyra, continues through monsoon planting, and stays with each plot for three years of care.

Field work

কোথা থেকে শুরুWhere the work begins

The restoration cycle is practical and local: women’s groups run community nurseries in Shyamnagar and Koyra, planting happens in the monsoon, and each planted plot stays in care for three years.

Step 01

Community nurseries

Nursery work begins with women’s groups in Shyamnagar and Koyra. The first donation level supports one nursery tray.

Step 02

Monsoon planting

Planting takes place in the monsoon, when volunteers can join the season from July through September.

Step 03

Three-year care

Each plot receives three years of care after planting. The largest donation tier funds a full season of that care.

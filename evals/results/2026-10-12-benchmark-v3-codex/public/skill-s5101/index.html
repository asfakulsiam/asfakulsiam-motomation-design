Field guide / Work

নার্সারি থেকে জোয়ারভাটা / From nursery to tide

The work is quiet and repeated: women’s groups run community nurseries in Shyamnagar and Koyra, planting happens in the monsoon, and each plot stays under care for three years.

নারীদের নার্সারি / Women-led nurseries

Shyamnagar and Koyra

Community nurseries are run by women’s groups in both places.

বর্ষায় রোপণ / Monsoon planting

Season sets the pace

Planting happens in the monsoon, when the landscape can take new roots.

তিন বছরের যত্ন / Three years of care

Care stays with each plot

Each planted plot receives three years of care after the monsoon planting.

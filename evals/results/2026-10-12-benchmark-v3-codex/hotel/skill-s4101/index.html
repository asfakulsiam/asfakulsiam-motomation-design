Boutique hotel · Shankhari Bazar
Haveli 1893

A 12-room stay in a restored merchant house in Old Dhaka, with rooftop breakfast above the old city's roofs and hotel-led walks through the neighbourhood.

Built in 1893 by a conch-shell merchant family. Restored in 2022 with the original doors, iron balconies and lime plaster kept in place.
Breakfast is included. The rooftop table is laid from 7–10am.
01 · The house

Merchant-house bones, kept visible.

Haveli 1893 sits in a restored 1890s merchant house in Puran Dhaka, on Shankhari Bazar.

The 2022 restoration kept the details that anchor the house to its lane: original doors, iron balconies and lime plaster.

03 · Rooftop

Breakfast above the old city's roofs.

Breakfast is served on the rooftop from 7–10am, with a view over the roofs of Old Dhaka.

The hour is practical and atmospheric at once: a meal included in the stay, laid above the lane before the day pulls outward.

7–10am · Breakfast included

The rooftop hour is the quietest room in the house without being a room at all: roofs, breakfast, and the city at the edge of the table.

Walks led by the hotel, in small groups.

The hotel leads walks through the neighbourhood and beyond, in small groups, over two to three hours.

The routes follow three precise invitations: Shankhari Bazar at dawn, Armanitola and the Star Mosque, and the river at Sadarghat.

Boutique hotel · Shankhari Bazar

A merchant house, kept open to the lane.

Haveli 1893 is a 12-room boutique hotel in a restored 1890s merchant house in Puran Dhaka. Original doors, iron balconies and lime plaster remain, while booking stays simple and breakfast is already included.

Threshold three

Walk out with the hotel, not away from it.

Haveli 1893 leads small-group walking tours through Old Dhaka. Each walk takes 2–3 hours and stays close to the districts that define the house’s setting.

At dawn in Shankhari Bazar

A hotel-led walk through the lane at first light, when the street’s scale and rhythm come forward.

Armanitola and the Star Mosque

A 2–3 hour route connecting nearby history and architecture in a small group.

Toward Sadarghat

The river becomes the final chapter, reached through the old city rather than around it.

Small-group pace

Every walk is kept to a small group, so the route remains guided rather than crowded.

Threshold four

Built in 1893. Restored in 2022.

The house began as the residence of a conch-shell merchant family in 1893. Its 2022 restoration kept the original doors, iron balconies and lime plaster, so the stay still moves through the old fabric of the building.

House origin
Merchant house built in 1893 by a conch-shell merchant family.
Restoration

The house

Built by a family of conch‑shell merchants.

1893Built
2022Restored

The house went up in 1893 for a conch-shell merchant family. Today it stands in Shankhari Bazar, in Puran Dhaka.

In 2022 it was restored, keeping three things of the original house.

  • i.Original doors
  • ii.Iron balconies
  • iii.Lime plaster

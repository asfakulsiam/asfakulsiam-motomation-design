The house

Built by a conch-shell merchant family

Haveli 1893 was built in 1893 by a family of conch-shell merchants in Shankhari Bazar, in Puran Dhaka. It was restored in 2022, and the restoration kept what the house already had.

  • Original doors
  • Iron balconies

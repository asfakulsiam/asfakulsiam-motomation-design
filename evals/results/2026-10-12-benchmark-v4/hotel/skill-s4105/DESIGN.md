# DESIGN.md: Haveli 1893

## Concept
- **Tension:** a 130-year-old house that must feel lived-in, while booking happens on an external engine
- **Concept sentence:** Every stay begins by opening the old door.
- **Archetype + mutation:** Timeline mutated by swapping the material (the room list becomes a row of openings)
- **Collision:** nakshi kantha → running stitch as the timeline axis
- **Signature move:** Aperture: scrubbed green doors open onto the lit courtyard
- **Dials:** ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2 → Tier 2
- **Rejected:** full-bleed courtyard photo with centred headline (template; no images allowed)

## Typography
Type mode: HOUSE STYLE (typography-led) — a hotel sells the feeling first; rates are plain tabular rows.

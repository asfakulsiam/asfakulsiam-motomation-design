# DESIGN: Haveli 1893

MOTOMATION PLAN
- Tension: a 130-year-old merchant house ↔ guests who book on a phone in two minutes.
- Concept: The site is the house in section: you climb from the street to the roof as you scroll, and the drawing lights one level at a time.
- Archetype: The Room, mutated by "a list becomes a map" (the room list is a section drawing you ascend).
- Collision: museum label → small precise caption (plate number, level, fact) beside every drawing.
- Signature: the Elevation rail. A fixed section drawing of the house; a marker climbs it as you read and each level fills when reached. Echoed by plate captions and the rising sun in the rooftop chapter.
- Dials: ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2 → Tier 2.
- Type mode: HOUSE STYLE (a hotel sells an impression first; prices and walks are set as short tabular rows).
- Type: Fraunces (warm old-style, soft stroke contrast, reads as heritage) / Switzer (neutral text).
- Palette: lime plaster #EFE7D8 / #E5DBC7, iron ink #1D1A16, muted #5B5144, one accent terracotta #9C3A1F (#E8895F on the dark rooftop chapter).
- Not doing: a photo carousel with a centred "Welcome" hero, an image slider of rooms, a map. No photos exist and none are invented.

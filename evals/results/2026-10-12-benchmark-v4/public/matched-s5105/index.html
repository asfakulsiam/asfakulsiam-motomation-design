ম্যানগ্রোভ ফিরিয়ে আনি Bringing the mangroves back

Sundari Trust restores mangroves in the Sundarbans buffer zone, working with local communities: women’s groups raise the seedlings, and every plot is cared for after it is planted.

Donate Volunteer to plant
140 hectares replanted since 2019 Source: Sundari Trust annual report 2025

নার্সারি থেকে বনFrom nursery to forest

Three steps, each led by the people who live beside the water.

  1. 1

    Community nurseries কমিউনিটি নার্সারি

    Women’s groups in Shyamnagar and Koyra run the nurseries where seedlings are raised.

  2. 2

    Planting in the monsoon বর্ষায় রোপণ

    Seedlings go into the plots during the monsoon, when the water and the season are with them.

  3. 3

    Three years of care তিন বছরের যত্ন

    Each plot is looked after for three years, so what is planted stays standing.

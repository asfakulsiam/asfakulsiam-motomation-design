Sundarbans buffer zone · Community mangrove restoration

ম্যানগ্রোভ ফিরিয়ে আনিBringing the mangroves back

Sundari Trust restores mangroves in the Sundarbans buffer zone with local communities: nurseries run by women's groups, planting in the monsoon, and three years of care for every plot.

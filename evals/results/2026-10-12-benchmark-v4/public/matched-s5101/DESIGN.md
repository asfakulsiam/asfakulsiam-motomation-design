# Sundari Trust: design notes

Concept: two languages, one shoreline. Bangla and English stand side by side like two banks of a tidal creek, and mangrove roots grow down between them.
Type mode: SERVICE STYLE for the forms and sections, with a large bilingual hero. Fraunces + Noto Serif Bengali for display, Hind Siliguri for text.
Palette: ink #0D241F, silt paper #F4EFE2, turmeric #E8A25A (on dark), clay #A8431A (on paper).
Motion: Tier 2. Roots draw into the waterline on load, a tide gauge tracks scroll, and each section has a different quiet entrance. Reduced motion shows everything static.

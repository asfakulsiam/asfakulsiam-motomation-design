Sundarbans buffer zone

ম্যানগ্রোভ ফিরিয়ে আনিBringing the mangroves back

Sundari Trust restores mangroves in the Sundarbans buffer zone with local communities, through nurseries run by women’s groups and three years of care for every plot.

আমাদের কাজ

Our work

  1. 01

    Community nurseriesকমিউনিটি নার্সারি

    Nurseries are run by women’s groups in Shyamnagar and Koyra. Seedlings are raised close to where they will be planted.

  2. 02

    Monsoon plantingবর্ষায় রোপণ

    Planting happens in the monsoon, when the plots are ready to receive young mangroves.

  3. 03

    Three years of careতিন বছরের যত্ন

    Each plot is looked after for three years after planting.

# DESIGN.md: Sundari Trust

## Concept
- **Tension:** Urgent funding need vs. work that takes three years per plot.
- **Concept sentence:** The tide comes in daily; a forest takes years. The page is a tide gauge you scroll up.
- **Archetype + mutation:** room mutated by wrong-room (a tide gauge / survey sheet structure).
- **Collision:** tide chart (sea) → periodic rise and fall, as a waterline that rises through the roots.
- **Signature move:** Waterline over drawn mangrove roots, scrubbed by scroll.
- **Dials:** ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 1 → Tier 2
- **Rejected:** Stock-photo hero, count-up on 140 (would show 0 before settling), catalogue and console archetypes.

## Typography
Type mode: SERVICE STYLE for the donate and volunteer forms, HOUSE STYLE for the hero. The visitor must act, so the hero word is big and the forms are compact.

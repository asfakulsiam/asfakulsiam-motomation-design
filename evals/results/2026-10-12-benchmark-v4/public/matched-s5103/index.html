ম্যানগ্রোভ ফিরিয়ে আনি Bringing the mangroves back

Sundari Trust restores mangroves in the Sundarbans buffer zone, working side by side with local communities.

Plate 02 · How a plot grows

নার্সারি থেকে বনFrom nursery to forest

Women's groups run the community nurseries in Shyamnagar and Koyra. We plant in the monsoon, and every plot is cared for over three years.

  1. 1

    Community nurseriesকমিউনিটি নার্সারি

    Women's groups in Shyamnagar and Koyra raise the seedlings in nursery trays, close to where they will be planted.

  2. 2

    Planting in the monsoonবর্ষায় রোপণ

    When the monsoon arrives, seedlings go into the ground, plot by plot and row by row, with the community.

  3. 3

    Three years of careতিন বছরের যত্ন

    Each plot is looked after for three years, so a planting becomes a standing mangrove and not just a date on a calendar.

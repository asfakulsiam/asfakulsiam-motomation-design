ম্যানগ্রোভ ফিরিয়ে আনিBringing the mangroves back

Sundari Trust restores mangroves in the Sundarbans buffer zone with local communities. Women's groups grow the seedlings, and every plot is cared for after planting.

Donate Volunteer
140hectares (১৪০ হেক্টর)

replanted since 2019. Source: Sundari Trust annual report 2025.

আমাদের কাজThe work

  1. 01 · নার্সারি / Nursery

    Women's groups grow the seedlings

    Community nurseries run by women's groups in Shyamnagar and Koyra raise the trays that every plot starts from.

  2. 02 · রোপণ / Planting

    Planted in the monsoon

    Seedlings go into the ground in the monsoon, with the communities who live beside the forest.

  3. 03 · যত্ন / Care

    Three years of care for each plot

    Planting is the start. Each plot is looked after for three years, which is what the replanted hectares count.

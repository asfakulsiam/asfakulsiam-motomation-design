CLI + web app for incidents

From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

    How it works

    Three steps, one of them is a link.

    1. 01

      Ingest

      $ tracewell ingest

      Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

    2. 02

      Ask why

      $ tracewell why --since 15m

      Lines are ranked by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

    3. 03

      Share

      $ explanation link

      Send the explanation link to the team, so everyone reads the same line.

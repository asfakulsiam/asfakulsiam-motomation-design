How it works

Ingest, ask why, share.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    tracewell ingest

    Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. 02

    Ask why

    tracewell why --since 15m

    Lines are ranked by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days. The strangest one comes first.

  3. 03

    Share the explanation

    explanation link

    Send the link to the team, so everyone reads the same line and the same reasoning.

Why it works

The line that explains an incident is often not the loudest one. It is the one that looks least like the last week.

Tracewell compares every line in your window with the previous 7 days and ranks by how unusual it is. You read the top of the list, not the whole stream.

# DESIGN: Tracewell

Tension: a flood of 4 million lines vs. the one line the engineer needs at 3am.
Concept: Everything is blurred noise until Tracewell pulls focus on the one line.
Archetype: Console, mutated by "invert the axis" (the page reads backwards from the incident: noise first, cause last).
Collision: darkroom enlarger (photography) -> focus knob and projected light; the accent is a safelight red.
Signature: Focus Pull. A wall of log lines sits blurred; the culprit line pulls sharp and lights up.
Dials: ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 2 -> Tier 2 (GSAP + ScrollTrigger).
Type mode: SERVICE STYLE. On-call engineers scan commands, logs and prices; the product frame is the content.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque (headings, a human voice against the mono) / Geist Mono (commands, logs, figures).
Palette: ground #0E0D0B, surface #1A1814, ink #F2EEE6, muted #A8A196, accent #E0442B (safelight; 4.65:1 on ground).
Not doing: logo wall, testimonials, usage numbers (none exist); centred hero with screenshot; purple gradient.

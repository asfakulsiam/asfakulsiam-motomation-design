CLI + web app for on-call engineers

From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell finds the one log line that explains an incident, by ranking every line on how unusual it is compared with the last 7 days.

Install TracewellSee it work

Three commands. One explanation.

No dashboards to build before the pager goes off.

  1. 01
    tracewell ingest

    Stream your logs in

    From files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. 02
    tracewell why --since 15m

    Ask what's unusual

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. 03
    explanation link

    Send the explanation

    Share the explanation link with the team.

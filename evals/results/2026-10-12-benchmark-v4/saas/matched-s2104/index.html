CLI + web app for incident response

From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell finds the single log line that explains your incident, by ranking every line on how unusual it is compared with the last 7 days. Built for the engineer who just got paged.

Install Tracewell See it find a line

How it works

Three steps. One explanation.

01

Ingest

$ tracewell ingest

Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

02

Ask why

$ tracewell why --since 15m

Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

03

Share

→ explanation link

Send the explanation link to the team, so everyone starts from the same line.

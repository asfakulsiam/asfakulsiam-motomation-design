From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell is a CLI and web app that finds the one log line that explains an incident.

03:41:07 ERROR payments-api pool exhausted: cannot acquire connection↳ not seen in the last 7 days

01 · how it works ↵

Three steps from incident to explanation.

  1. 1tracewell ingest

    Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. 2tracewell why --since 15m

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. 3share the link

    Send the explanation link to the team.

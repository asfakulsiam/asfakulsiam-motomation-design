CLI + web app for incident response

From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell ranks every line by how unusual it is compared with the last 7 days, so the line that explains the incident comes first. Built for on-call backend engineers.

$ brew install tracewell

or

$ curl -fsSL https://tracewell.dev/install.sh | sh
tracewell why --since 15m4,000,000 lines

How it works

Three steps, one command each.

  1. Stream your logs in

    tracewell ingest

    Read logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes. Nothing to reformat first.

  2. Ask what's unusual

    tracewell why --since 15m

    Lines are ranked by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days. The most unusual line is at the top.

  3. Share the explanation

    explanation link

    Send the link to the team, so everyone looks at the same line and the same reasoning.

The product

The terminal asks. The result view explains.

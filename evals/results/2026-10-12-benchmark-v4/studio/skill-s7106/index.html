Process

Four steps, in this order
  1. I

    Site visit

    We go to the plot first, and look at where the light falls.

  2. II

    Sketch

    Plan and courtyard are drawn by hand before anything else.

  3. III

    Brick sample

    The brick is chosen in the hand, not on a screen.

  4. IV

    Build

    The house is built from the drawings.

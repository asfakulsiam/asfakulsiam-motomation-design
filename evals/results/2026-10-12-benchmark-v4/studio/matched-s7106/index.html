The studio

Process

From the ground up, in four moves.

  1. Site visit

    We begin on the land, reading its light and its weather.

  2. Sketch

    The idea is drawn by hand, around the court.

  3. Brick sample

    A sample is made so the material is judged in daylight.

  4. Build

    The house is built, brick by brick.

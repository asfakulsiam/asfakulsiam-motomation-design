Process

From the site to the brick

  1. Site visit

    We start on the plot: sun, trees, water, neighbours.

  2. Sketch

    Plan and section by hand, until the courtyard is right.

  3. Brick sample

    A small wall is laid before the big one.

  4. Build

    The house goes up brick by brick, to the sketch.

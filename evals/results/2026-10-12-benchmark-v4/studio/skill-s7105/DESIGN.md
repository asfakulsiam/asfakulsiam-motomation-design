# DESIGN.md: Ittaghor Studio

## Concept
- **Tension:** The partners' real work is the drawing; visitors judge a house by its picture.
- **Concept sentence:** Each house is shown twice, as image and as plan, and scrolling withdraws the image until only the plan is left.
- **Archetype + mutation:** Diptych, mutated by "reverse the reveal: start complete and take away as you scroll".
- **Collision:** Darkroom tray run backwards, plus a theatre curtain: the image withdraws sideways.
- **Signature move:** The withdrawing image edge (hero brick wall, then five projects).
- **Dials:** ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2 → Tier 2
- **Rejected:** Photo grid + carousel; full-bleed pinned walkthrough (Tier 5 too heavy for the content).

## Typography
Type mode: HOUSE STYLE (typography-led), because a studio portfolio is judged on name, voice and the work.

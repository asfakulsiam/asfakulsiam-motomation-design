# DESIGN.md: Ittaghor Studio

## Concept
- **Tension:** brick is heavy and closed; the practice sells light and an open court.
- **Concept sentence:** A site that starts as a solid brick wall and cuts the courtyard out of it as you scroll, so the light arrives.
- **Archetype + mutation:** Field Guide (entries with illustrations) mutated by reversing the reveal: the wall starts complete and material is taken away.
- **Collision:** blueprint (architecture) → cream lines on dark ground with dimension lines that draw in.
- **Signature move:** courtyard-cut: a void opens in the hero wall; each plan then draws itself and its court fills with light.
- **Dials:** ENERGY 2 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2 → Tier 2
- **Rejected:** a pinned film of the five houses (Tier 5), because the concept needs one gesture, not a ride; a generic image grid of facades.

## Typography
Type mode: HOUSE STYLE (typography-led), because a studio portfolio sells taste and the positioning line is the hero.
Fraunces (display, warm) / Instrument Sans (text) / DM Mono (labels, plan captions).

The firing story

Stacked, fired with mango wood, then left to cool

Rina and Sohel fire 20–40 pieces at a time, and the kiln sets the pace. Scroll and the bar below moves through it, drawn to scale.

  1. 3 days

    Stacking

    The kiln is loaded by hand, piece by piece.

  2. 36 hours

    Firing with mango wood

    Flame and ash move through the stack and mark every piece differently.

  3. 4 days

    Cooling

    The kiln stays closed until it has cooled.

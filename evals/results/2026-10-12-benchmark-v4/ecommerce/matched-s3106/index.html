    The firing

    Eight days for a firing, and most of it is waiting.

    Each batch is stacked by hand, fired with mango wood, then left alone to cool. What the flame does in between is what you see on the cup.

    1. 3days

      Stacking

      The kiln is loaded piece by piece, 20–40 to a firing.

    2. 36hours

      Firing

      Fed with mango wood.

    3. 4days

      Cooling

      The kiln stays shut until it has cooled.

    Bar widths are to scale: 3 days, 36 hours (a day and a half) and 4 days.

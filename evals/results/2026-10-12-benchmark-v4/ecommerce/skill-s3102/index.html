The firing story

Every cup gets its mark from one long firing.

  1. Stacking 3 days

    The kiln is stacked with the whole batch of 20–40 pieces before a single flame is lit.

  2. Firing 36 hours

    The kiln is fed with mango wood. The flame and its ash mark each piece differently.

  3. Cooling 4 days

    The kiln cools slowly. Then the cups come out, each one marked differently.

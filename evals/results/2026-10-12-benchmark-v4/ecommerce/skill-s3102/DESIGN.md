# DESIGN: Mati ceramics shop

Tension: handmade and unrepeatable (every cup marked differently) vs a shop that must be fast and certain.
Concept: Every cup is a record of its firing, so the shop is an index you can play back.
Archetype: Index, mutated (list becomes a map): the price list is the page, and the firing is a dial you scrub.
Collision: vinyl record (music), lending the rotating dial and the needle that drops into chapters.
Signature: Firing Dial, a pinned circular scrub through 3 days of stacking, 36 hours of firing, 4 days of cooling.
Dials: ENERGY 1 · RHYTHM 2 · MOTION 2, Tier 2.
Type mode: SERVICE STYLE for the shop and cart (compare, read, buy); the firing chapter is the one large-type moment.
Type: Newsreader (display, warm and bookish) / Hanken Grotesk (text) / Noto Sans Bengali only for the ৳ glyph.
Palette: bone #EFE9DF, kiln ink #1C1815, ember #A83A16 (one accent, flame only, plus the buy button).

The firing story

Ten days around one fire.

Every batch of 20–40 pieces follows the same slow path: stacking the kiln, feeding it mango wood, then waiting for it to cool.

  1. 3days of stacking
  2. 36hours of firing with mango wood
  3. 4days of cooling

Bars are drawn to the same time scale: 3 days, 36 hours (a day and a half), 4 days.

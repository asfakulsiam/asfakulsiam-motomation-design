    The firing

    Eight and a half days to make one batch.

    Every firing holds 20–40 pieces. The next one is on 18 November, with 32 pieces.

    1. 3days

      Stacking

      The kiln is stacked piece by piece with the whole batch: cups, plates and vases.

    2. 36hours

      Firing with mango wood

      The flame runs for 36 hours. Ash settles on the stoneware and melts into the glaze, and the flame marks each piece its own way.

    3. 4days

      Cooling

      Four days of cooling come before the first piece comes out.

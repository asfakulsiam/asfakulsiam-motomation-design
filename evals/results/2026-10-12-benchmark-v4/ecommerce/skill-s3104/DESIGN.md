# DESIGN: Mati

Tension: handmade and unrepeatable vs. a shop that must sell a repeatable product.
Concept: Every cup is marked by the flame, so the shop is built around one cup and the 8.5 days of fire behind it.
Archetype: catalogue, mutated by single object (the cup is the hero, the cart icon and the rail all refer to it).
Collision: museum label (collide.mjs seed 3104, draw 2) lends the spec sheet and margin captions.
Signature: timecode rail, a sticky 3 : 1.5 : 4 day rail that fills as the firing story scrolls.
Dials: ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 2 (tier 2).
Type mode: SERVICE STYLE for the shop rows and price (compare and act), one display word only in the hero and firing numerals. Instrument Serif / Hanken Grotesk (tabular figures) / Noto Sans Bengali for the taka sign.
Palette: ash paper #EFE8DB, ink #211A15, ember #A63A18 (5.31:1), kiln #1B1511 with glow #F08A55 (7.29:1).

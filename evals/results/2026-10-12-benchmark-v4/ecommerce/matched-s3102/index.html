The firing

Eight and a half days for one batch of 20–40 pieces.

3 daysStacking
36 hFiring

01

Stacking

Three days of stacking the kiln, piece by piece, so the flame can travel between them.

02

Firing

Thirty-six hours of firing with mango wood. The ash lands on the stoneware and melts into the glaze.

03

Cooling

Four days of cooling before the kiln is opened. Only then do we see what the fire did.

01 / How it works

Three commands. No dashboards to build.

(01)

Stream your logs in.

tracewell ingest

Point it at files, Docker or Kubernetes. Tracewell keeps the history it needs to know what normal looks like.

1Sources: log files, Docker containers, Kubernetes. Retention depends on your plan: 3 days on Free, 30 on Team.

(02)

Ask what's strange.

tracewell why --since 15m

Every line from the last 15 minutes is ranked by how unusual it is. The line you'd have found at 3 a.m., eventually, comes first.

2"Unusual" is measured against the last 7 days of your own logs, not a generic model of what errors look like.

(03)

Send the explanation, not a screenshot.

share the explanation link

One link shows the ranked lines and the one that explains the incident. Your team opens it and sees the same thing you did.

3Shared incidents are part of the Team plan.

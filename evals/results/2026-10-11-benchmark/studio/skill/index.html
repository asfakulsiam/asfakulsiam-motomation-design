The studio.

Dhaka · Founded 2011

Ittaghor Studio is a three-partner architecture practice in Dhaka, known for brick and courtyard houses. We design around light, and we start from the brick.

How a house gets made.

Four steps

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Brick sample

    A real brick, held in the real light, before anything is ordered.

  4. 04

    Build

Room 02The house

Built in 1893 by a family of conch-shell merchants.

The house stands in Shankhari Bazar, the old quarter of the shell-cutters. In 2022 it was restored, and the restoration kept what was already there rather than replacing it.

You walk in through the original doors. You sleep behind them.

The house

Built for conch-shell merchants.

Haveli 1893 was built in 1893 by a conch-shell merchant family, in the Shankhari Bazar quarter of Puran Dhaka. It was restored in 2022, keeping the original doors, iron balconies and lime plaster.

Today it is a hotel of twelve rooms.

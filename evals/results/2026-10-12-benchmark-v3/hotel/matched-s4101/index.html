The house

Built 1893 by a family of conch-shell merchants.

Haveli 1893 stood in Shankhari Bazar long before it took guests. In 2022 it was restored, and the work was to keep, not replace.

You still walk through the original doors, step onto the iron balconies and touch lime plaster walls.

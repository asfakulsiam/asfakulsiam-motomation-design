The house

Built to trade conch shells.

Haveli 1893 was built in 1893 by a conch-shell merchant family, in the lanes of Shankhari Bazar. In 2022 it was restored, and the work was a matter of keeping: the original doors, the iron balconies and the lime plaster are still the house.

  • 1893Built by a conch-shell merchant family.
  • 2022Restored, keeping the original doors, iron balconies and lime plaster.

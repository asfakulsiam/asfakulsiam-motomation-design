The house

Built by a conch-shell merchant family

The house was built in 1893 by a conch-shell merchant family. In 2022 it was restored, keeping the original doors, iron balconies and lime plaster.

Today it is a hotel of twelve rooms around a courtyard, on Shankhari Bazar.

The house

A merchant’s house that was kept, not replaced.

1893Built
2022Restored

Haveli 1893 was built by a conch-shell merchant family in the Shankhari Bazar quarter of Puran Dhaka. In 2022 the house was restored, keeping its original doors, iron balconies and lime plaster.

Those three things are still here.

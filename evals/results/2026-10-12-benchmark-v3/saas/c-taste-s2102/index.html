How it works

Ingest, ask why, share the link.

Bring logs from where they already live.

Tracewell reads what your services already write. Nothing to rewrite or re-instrument.

filesDockerKubernetes
  1. tracewell ingest

    Streams logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. tracewell why --since 15m

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. share the explanation link

    Send the link to the team so everyone reads the same line and the same reasoning.

The answer, ranked and explained.

The web app shows the top line first, with the lines around it for context.

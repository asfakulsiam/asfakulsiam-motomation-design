From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell ranks your logs by how unusual they are, so the line that explains the incident comes first.

$ brew install tracewell

or curl -fsSL https://tracewell.dev/install.sh | sh

Three steps from page to cause.

  1. tracewell ingest

    Stream your logs

    Read from files, Docker or Kubernetes. Nothing to rewrite in your services.

  2. tracewell why --since 15m

    Ask what's unusual

    Every line from the last 15 minutes is ranked against the previous 7 days. Routine noise sinks. New behavior rises.

  3. explanation link

    Share the answer

    Send the explanation link to your team so everyone starts from the same line.

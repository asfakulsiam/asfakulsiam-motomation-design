How it works

Three steps, from raw logs to a link.

  1. Ingest

    tracewell ingest

    Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. Ask why

    tracewell why --since 15m

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. Share

    explanation link

    Send the explanation link to the team. Everyone sees the same line and the same reasoning.

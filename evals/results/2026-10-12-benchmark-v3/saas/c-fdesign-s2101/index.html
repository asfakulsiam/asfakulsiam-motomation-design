From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell is a CLI and web app for on-call engineers. It ranks your logs by how unusual they are, so the line that explains the incident comes first.

Install Tracewell See it run

Three steps from page to explanation

  1. Stream your logs in

    tracewell ingest

    Read from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. Ask what went wrong

    tracewell why --since 15m

    Lines are ranked by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. Share the explanation

    explanation link

    Send one link to the team, so everyone starts from the same line.

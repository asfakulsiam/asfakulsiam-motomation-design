From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell is a CLI and web app that finds the one log line that explains an incident. Built for on-call backend engineers.

Install TracewellSee it work

Three steps, from page to explanation

Point Tracewell at your logs, ask what went wrong, send the answer to your team.

  1. Ingest

    tracewell ingest streams logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. Ask why

    tracewell why --since 15m ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. Share

    Share the explanation link with the team, so everyone sees the same line.

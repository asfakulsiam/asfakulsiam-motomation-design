Three steps. One answer.

Point it at your logs, ask what changed, send the answer to your team.

  1. tracewell ingest

    Stream your logs

    Read from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. tracewell why --since 15m

    Ask why

    Lines are ranked by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. explanation link

    Share it

    Send the explanation link so the whole team sees the same line.

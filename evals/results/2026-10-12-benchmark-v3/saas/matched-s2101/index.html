CLI + web app for incident response

From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell finds the one log line that explains an incident. Built for on-call backend engineers who would rather read one line than grep a million.

$ brew install tracewell
or
$ curl -fsSL https://tracewell.dev/install.sh | sh

01 — How it works

Three steps from page to explanation.

  1. STEP 1

    Ingest

    tracewell ingest

    Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. STEP 2

    Ask why

    tracewell why --since 15m

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. STEP 3

    Share

    explanation link

    Send the explanation link to the team, so everyone reads the same line.

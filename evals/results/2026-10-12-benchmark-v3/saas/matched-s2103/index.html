CLI + web app for incident response

From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell finds the one log line that explains an incident. Built for on-call backend engineers who would rather fix it than scroll.

How it works

Three steps, from stream to shared answer.

  1. 01 · INGEST

    Stream your logs in

    tracewell ingest

    Read from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. 02 · ASK

    Ask why

    tracewell why --since 15m

    Lines are ranked by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. 03 · SHARE

    Send the explanation

    → explanation link

    Share the explanation link with the team, so everyone reads the same line.

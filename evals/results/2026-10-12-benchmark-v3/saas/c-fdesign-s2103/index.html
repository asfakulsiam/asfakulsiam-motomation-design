From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell is a CLI and web app that ranks your logs by how unusual they are, so you can see what explains the incident.

brew install tracewell

Three commands from page to explanation.

  1. tracewell ingest

    Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. tracewell why --since 15m

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. Share the link

    Send the explanation link to the team, so everyone looks at the same line.

See the line, not the haystack.

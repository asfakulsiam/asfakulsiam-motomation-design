From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

Tracewell is a CLI and web app that finds the one log line that explains an incident. Built for on-call backend engineers.

brew install tracewell
curl -fsSL https://tracewell.dev/install.sh | sh

Three steps, no digging

  1. 01tracewell ingest

    Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

  2. 02tracewell why --since 15m

    Ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

  3. 03share the link

    Send the explanation link to the team.

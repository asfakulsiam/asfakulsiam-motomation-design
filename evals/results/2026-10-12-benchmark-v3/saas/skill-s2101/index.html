    01 · How it works

    Three steps, and you only read the last one.

    Ingest once, ask what changed, hand the answer to whoever is awake next.

    STEP 1

    Stream your logs in

    $ tracewell ingest

    Read logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

    STEP 2

    Ask why

    $ tracewell why --since 15m

    Lines are ranked by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days. The strangest line comes first.

    STEP 3

    Share the link

    → explanation link

    Send the explanation link to the team, so they start from the same line you did.

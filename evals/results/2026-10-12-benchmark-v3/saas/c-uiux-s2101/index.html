From 4 million log lines to the one that matters.

How it works

    
Three steps from page to explanation.

    
    
      
  1. 
        
    Ingest your logs
    
        
    Stream logs from files, Docker or Kubernetes.
    
        $ tracewell ingest
      
    2. 
      
  2. 
        
    Ask why
    
        
    Tracewell ranks lines by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.
    
        $ tracewell why --since 15m
      
    3. 
      
  3. 
        
    Share the link
    
        
    Send the explanation link to the team, so everyone looks at the same line.
    
        → explanation link
      
    4.

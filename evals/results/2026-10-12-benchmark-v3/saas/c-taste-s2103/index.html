Nobody reads 4 million lines at 3 a.m.

Tracewell reads them for you, then shows the one that changed the most. Send the link to your team and everyone sees the same explanation.

Three commands from alert to answer

  1. Stream your logs

    Ingest from files, Docker or Kubernetes.

    $tracewell ingest

  2. Ask what went wrong

    Lines are ranked by how unusual they are compared with the last 7 days.

    $tracewell why --since 15m

  3. Share the explanation

    Send the explanation link to the team so everyone starts from the same line.

    >Paste the explanation link in chat

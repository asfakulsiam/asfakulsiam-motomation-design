How a house gets built

  1. Site visit

    We walk the plot and watch where the light falls.

  2. Sketch

    Plan and courtyard are drawn together by hand.

  3. Brick sample

    A test of brick and bond is laid before anything is ordered.

  4. Build

    The house goes up on site, wall by wall.

The studio

Ittaghor Studio is a three-partner practice in Dhaka. Since 2011 we have designed houses in brick, arranged around courtyards that bring in light and air.

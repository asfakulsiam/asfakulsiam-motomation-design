Process

From the site to the wall.

  1. Site visit

    We stand on the plot and read its light, its trees and its water.

  2. Sketch

    The first drawing is a plan: where the courtyard opens and where the walls close.

  3. Brick sample

    We lay up a sample of the brick before the wall is drawn in full.

  4. Build

    The house is built from the plan, in brick.

How a firing goes

3 days

Stacking

Every cup, plate and vase is placed in the kiln by hand.

36 hours

Firing

Fed with mango wood.

4 days

Cooling

The kiln stays shut until it is cool.

Each firing holds 20–40 pieces, so every batch is small. The width of each block matches its length of time.

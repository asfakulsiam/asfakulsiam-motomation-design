      How a firing goes

      Most of a firing is waiting.

      Mati is two people, Rina and Sohel, in Rajshahi. A firing holds 20–40 pieces, and the fire marks every one differently.

      1. First

        3 days of stacking

        The kiln is filled piece by piece.

      2. Then

        36 hours of firing

        Fed with mango wood.

      3. Last

        4 days of cooling

        Then the kiln is opened.

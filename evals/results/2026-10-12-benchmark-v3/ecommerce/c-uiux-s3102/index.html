The firing story

Small batches, slow fire

Rina and Sohel fire 20–40 pieces at a time. Every batch follows the same slow rhythm.

  1. 3
    days of stacking

    Cups, plates and vases are loaded into the kiln by hand.

  2. 36
    hours of firing

    The kiln is fed with mango wood the whole time.

  3. 4
    days of cooling

    The kiln closes and cools before it is opened.

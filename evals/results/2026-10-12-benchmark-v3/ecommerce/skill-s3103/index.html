    One firing, start to finish

    3 days · 36 hours · 4 days

    Stack · 3 days

    1. Step 1

      Stack

      3 days of stacking. The kiln is packed with one batch of 20–40 pieces.

    2. Step 2

      Fire

      36 hours of firing with mango wood. Flame and ash glaze each piece in its own way.

    3. Step 3

      Cool

      4 days of cooling. Then the cups come out, each one marked differently. Next firing: 18 November, 32 pieces.

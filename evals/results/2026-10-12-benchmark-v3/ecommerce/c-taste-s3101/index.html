How a firing goes

Every batch of 20-40 pieces goes through the same three stages in the kiln.

3days

Stacking

Pieces are set into the kiln by hand, so the flame can reach each one.

36hours

Firing with mango wood

Ash from the wood settles on the pots and melts into the glaze.

4days

Cooling

The kiln stays closed until it has cooled. Then Rina and Sohel open it.

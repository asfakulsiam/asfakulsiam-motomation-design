The firing story

Eight days to make a batch of cups.

Nothing here can be hurried. The kiln is stacked by hand, burned with mango wood through a day and a half, then left alone to cool.

  1. 3 days

    Stacking

    Every piece is placed by hand so the flame can travel between them.

  2. 36 hours

    Firing

    The kiln burns mango wood without a break. The ash settles on the clay and becomes glaze.

  3. 4 days

    Cooling

    We wait. Opening early would risk the whole batch.

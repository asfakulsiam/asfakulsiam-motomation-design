What we do

আমাদের কাজNurseries, planting, care

Mangroves return when the people who live beside them lead the work.

01

Community nurseries

কমিউনিটি নার্সারি

Women's groups run our nurseries in Shyamnagar and Koyra, raising saplings in trays before they go to the shore.

02

Planting in the monsoon

বর্ষায় রোপণ

Saplings go into the ground during the monsoon, plot by plot, row by row, with volunteers and local planters.

03

Three years of care

তিন বছরের যত্ন

Every plot is looked after for three years, so what we plant has the chance to grow into forest.

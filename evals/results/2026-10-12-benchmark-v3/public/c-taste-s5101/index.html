আমাদের কাজOur work

Local women lead the nurseries. Each plot is looked after until the trees can stand on their own.

Community nurseriesকমিউনিটি নার্সারি

Planting in the monsoonবর্ষায় রোপণ

Seedlings go into the ground during the monsoon, when the tidal land is wet and ready.

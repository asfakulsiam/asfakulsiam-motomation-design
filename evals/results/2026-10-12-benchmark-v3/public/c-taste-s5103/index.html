আমাদের কাজ Our work

  1. নার্সারিGrow

    Women's groups run community nurseries in Shyamnagar and Koyra, raising the seedlings that restore the buffer zone.

  2. রোপণPlant

    Seedlings go into the ground in the monsoon, planted with the communities who live beside the forest.

  3. যত্নCare

    Each plot is looked after for three years, so the young mangroves have time to take hold.

What we do

আমাদের কাজOur work

Community nurseries

Women's groups in Shyamnagar and Koyra run the nurseries where seedlings are raised.

Planting in the monsoon

Seedlings go into the ground during the monsoon, alongside local communities.

Three years of care

Every plot is cared for over three years, so the young mangroves have time to take hold.

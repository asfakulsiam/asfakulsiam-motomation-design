What we do

আমাদের কাজ Our work

Community nurseries

Women's groups in Shyamnagar and Koyra run the nurseries where every seedling starts.

Planting in the monsoon

Seedlings go into the mud when the rains arrive, planted alongside local communities.

Three years of care

Each plot is looked after for three years after planting.

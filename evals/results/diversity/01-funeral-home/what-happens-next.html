Step by step

What happens next

  1. The first hour

    Call the line. A person answers, not a menu. Tell us where your family member is and which rite you follow.

  2. We come to you

    Two of us arrive with a vehicle, at home or at the hospital. We bring the papers you will need to sign.

  3. The washing

    Ghusl or snan, done by people of your faith, men for men and women for women. Family may join.

  4. The rite

    Burial at the graveyard you choose, cremation at the ghat, or a church service. We arrange the place and the time.

  5. After

    Transport to the home district if the family wishes, and the death certificate paperwork with the city office.

  6. Paperwork

    We fill in the death registration with you and take it to the city office. You keep the copies.

  7. Later

    If you want a gathering on the fortieth day or a shraddha, we can help with the hall and the food.

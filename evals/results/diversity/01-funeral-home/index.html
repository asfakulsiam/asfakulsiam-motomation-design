Instructions for the family · keep this page

We will come to you, tonight if you need us.

Shanti Ghat has looked after families in Narayanganj since 1974: Muslim, Hindu and Christian. Below is everything that happens next, in order, and what each part costs.

What happens next

  1. The first hour

    Call the line. A person answers, not a menu. Tell us where your family member is and which rite you follow.

  2. Within 90 minutes

    Two of us arrive with a vehicle, at home or at the hospital. We bring the papers you will need to sign [NEEDS COPY: arrival time].

  3. The washing

    Ghusl or snan, done by people of your faith, men for men and women for women. Family may join.

  4. The rite

    Burial at the graveyard you choose, cremation at the ghat, or a church service. We arrange the place and the time.

  5. After

    Transport to the home district if the family wishes, and the death certificate paperwork with the city office.

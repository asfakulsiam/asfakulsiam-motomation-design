    First visit · about 30 minutes

    The first visit, page by page

    Read this with your child the night before. Each part below is a page in their passport.

    Hello

    Arrive and meet Bubbles

    Come ten minutes early. Your child can feed the fish while you fill in one short form.

    Chair

    Ride the chair

    The chair goes up and down. Your child presses the button first.

    Count

    Count the teeth

    We count them out loud with a small mirror. Most children have 20 baby teeth.

    Brush

    A tickle brush

    A soft spinning brush polishes the teeth. It tickles. Then a fluoride paint if your child is over two.

    Stamp

    The first stamp

    Your child picks the stamp colour. You get a short note on what we saw and when to come back.

    Children's dentistry · Uttara · ages 1 to 14

    Every visit gets a stamp.

    At Little Molars a check-up is a page in your child's tooth passport. We tell them what we will do, show them the tool, and then do it. No surprises, no white coats.

    Book a first check-upWhat the first visit looks like

    The fish tank in our waiting room. Its name is Bubbles.

    Tell

    Tell

    We explain each step in words a four-year-old knows. "Counting your teeth" comes before any tool comes out.

    Show

    Show

    Your child holds the mirror, feels the soft brush on a fingertip, and hears the water spray before it goes near a tooth.

    Do

    Do

    Only then do we start, and a raised hand means stop. Every time. Children learn that they are in charge of the chair.

    Stamp

    Stamp

    At the end, a new stamp in the passport. Ten stamps and the passport goes on the clinic wall with their name.

Reading your bill

  1. Your account number is at the top left. Keep it for every payment.
  2. Usage is the difference between this month's and last month's meter reading, in thousands of litres.
  3. Pay by the due date shown on the bill to avoid a late fee.

Where to pay

At any ward office counter, at a partner bank, or by mobile banking with the account number printed on your bill.

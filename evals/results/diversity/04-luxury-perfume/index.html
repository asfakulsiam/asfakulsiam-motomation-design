Maison Ilham · Gulshan

Oud No. 7

Seven years to make. One hundred and twenty bottles a year. Read it backwards, from today to the grove.

Year seven. Today.

Bottle 41 of 120 is the next to be filled. 79 remain for this year [NEEDS COPY: confirm count].

Year six.

The vetiver is smoked over coconut husk and laid into the oil. The rose has already gone in.

Year five.

Rose from Savar, picked before seven in the morning, distilled the same day.

Year four.

The oil is tasted, not tested. If it is not ready, it waits another year.

Year three.

Copper vessels, sealed with cloth and clay, in a room kept dark.

Year two.

Nothing happens that anyone can see. This is most of the work.

Year one.

The wood is distilled slowly over eleven days.

Year zero.

Agarwood cut from one grove in Sujanagar, Moulvibazar, from trees older than the house.

Three notes

  1. OudSujanagar agarwood, seven years
  2. RoseSavar damask, same-day distilled

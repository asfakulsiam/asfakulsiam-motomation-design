The oud

One grove, seven years

Agarwood forms when the aquilaria tree defends a wound with resin. It cannot be hurried, only waited for.

The grove

Sujanagar in Moulvibazar has grown agar for perfume for four hundred years [NEEDS COPY: source]. Our wood comes from one family's grove.

The still

Eleven days of slow distillation in copper, the water changed by hand.

The wait

Seven years in sealed vessels. The oil darkens from straw to amber to the colour of tea.

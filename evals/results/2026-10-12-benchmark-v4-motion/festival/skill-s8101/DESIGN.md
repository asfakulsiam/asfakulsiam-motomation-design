# DESIGN.md: Chattogram festival, 14–15 February

Tension: 18 artists on two stages in two days ↔ a visitor who can only look at one name at a time.
Concept: A dark field with two follow-spots; scroll walks the light from name to name, so the line-up is read one lit act at a time.
Archetype: timeline (two days on one axis), mutated by print marginalia (margin notes, folios).
Collision: library card catalogue → tickets are index cards with tabs.
Signature: follow-spot. Two beams from the top corners converge on the line-up row nearest the middle of the screen. The lit row brightens and nudges right.
Dials: ENERGY 3 · RHYTHM 3 · MOTION 3 → Tier 4 (forced). The concept is lit and spatial, so one WebGL canvas is justified.

Type mode: HOUSE STYLE (event poster; type is the image).
Type: Big Shoulders Display 900 (condensed poster face) / Instrument Sans (text) / Noto Sans Bengali only for ৳.
Palette: night #0B0A0F, bone #EDE7DA, dim bone #B3AEA2, one accent amber #FFB547 (the spot).
